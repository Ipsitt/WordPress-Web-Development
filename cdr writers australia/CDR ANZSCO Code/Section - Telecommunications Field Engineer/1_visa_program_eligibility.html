Visa Program Eligibility

Because 313212 is featured on the Short-term Skilled Occupation List (STSOL), its immigration pathways target state-nominated, regional provisional, and temporary employer-sponsored streams:

Eligible Visas:

Subclass 190 (Skilled Nominated)
Subclass 491 (Work Regional - State Nominated)
Subclass 482 (Skills in Demand - Short-Term Stream)
Subclass 494 (Regional Employer Sponsored)
Subclass 407 (Training Visa)

Ineligible Programs & Streams:

Subclass 189 (Skilled Independent) — Ineligible due to Short-Term list restriction
Subclass 186 (ENS Direct Entry) — Direct permanent pathway closed for Short-Term profiles
Subclass 482 (Core Skills Stream) — Restricted to occupations on the MLTSSL
Subclass 489 / Subclass 187 (RSMS) — Permanently closed and superseded

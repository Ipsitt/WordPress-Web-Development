What is the AACA skill assessment for architects?

The Architects Accreditation Council of Australia (AACA) is the body that assesses architects for Australian skilled migration. An AACA skill assessment checks whether your architecture qualification and competencies match Australian standards for the occupation Architect (OSCA 241131, previously ANZSCO 232111).

You may also see it called an AACA skills assessment or AACA migration skills assessment. It is separate from registering as an architect, which is handled by each State or Territory Architects Registration Board.

Which AACA skill assessment pathway fits you?

Your qualification decides which AACA skills assessment route applies. Pick the one that matches yours.

Overseas Qualifications Assessment (OQA)

If your architecture qualification was awarded outside Australia, the OQA is the main route. Since March 2022, new applicants complete the qualification check and the competency assessment in one application. A Suitable outcome also gives you a Skilled Migration Assessment on request.

Verification of an accredited qualification

If you hold an accredited Master of Architecture from Australia, New Zealand or Hong Kong, you do not need an OQA. You can apply for a Verification of Australian Accredited Architecture Qualification to obtain your Skilled Migration Assessment. Check AACA's accredited list first.

No OQA neededLower feeCheck the accredited list

Experienced Practitioner Assessment (EPA)

The EPA is the alternative to the OQA for an overseas-qualified architect seeking a Skilled Migration Assessment. AACA publishes separate fees for local, overseas and modified overseas applicants, so confirm eligibility on its EPA page before you choose.

OQA Stage 2 only

Applicants with a successful OQA Stage 1 outcome issued before 1 March 2022 can apply for Stage 2 only, and must submit evidence of that outcome. Without it, a new application is needed to obtain a Skilled Migration Assessment.

AACA also runs mutual recognition agreements with several countries. These mainly concern registration, so check AACA before assuming they replace a migration assessment.

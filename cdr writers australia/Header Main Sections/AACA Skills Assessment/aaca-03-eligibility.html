AACA skills assessment eligibility for overseas architects

Four conditions decide whether your AACA OQA application is ready to lodge.

5-year

Qualification length

A 5-year architecture course is the expected standard, and it must permit registration as an architect in the country that awarded it.

6 months

Paid experience

At least six months of paid, post-graduate architectural work, excluding practice built into your course.

English

Language proof

Evidence is needed if your architecture program was not taught in English.

Anywhere

Where you apply from

You can apply whether you live in Australia or overseas.

What goes into an AACA competency application

Beyond your degree, AACA assesses four pieces of evidence together.

01

A portfolio of 3 to 4 projects

AACA accepts up to four projects. Include one complex project, typically medium or larger scale. A final-year or thesis design project is recommended because it gives a solid base for interview discussion, though post-graduate professional projects alone are acceptable.

02

NSCA summary statements

Your statements must address the 13 topic areas and their performance criteria in the 2021 NSCA, supported by drawings, sketches or other documents from your portfolio projects.

03

Work experience evidence

Provide at least six months full-time equivalent of post-graduate architectural work, with a detailed reference letter. Practice built into your course does not count.

04

English language evidence

Needed when your program was not taught in English. AACA accepts a standard English test, or a letter from an Australian architectural employer confirming your English is adequate.

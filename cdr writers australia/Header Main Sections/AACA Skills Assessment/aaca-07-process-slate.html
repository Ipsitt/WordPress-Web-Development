How the AACA assessment process works

Six steps from choosing your pathway to receiving your outcome letter.

Confirm your pathway

Check whether the OQA, Verification or EPA applies, then read the OQA Applicants Guide.

Prepare a complete application

Finish your portfolio, statements and evidence first. AACA does not take documents in stages.

Apply online and pay

Lodge through the AACA applicant portal and pay by Visa or Mastercard.

Document review and feedback

AACA gives one round of email feedback. You revise and resubmit within the time limits.

Competency Assessment Interview

Assessors discuss your portfolio and competencies with you. Know every project you submit.

Receive your outcome

A Suitable outcome brings an outcome letter and, on request, a Skilled Migration Assessment.

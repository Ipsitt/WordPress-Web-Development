AACA migration assessment vs architect registration

A successful AACA assessment opens two separate doors. Do not confuse them.

≠
Skilled Migration Assessment
  • Supports a skilled migration application for the occupation Architect, OSCA 241131.
  • Valid for three years from the date of issue.
  • Can be renewed within about 10 working days.
  • Visa eligibility and points are decided separately by Home Affairs.
Architect registration
  • A Suitable outcome lets you apply for the three-part Architectural Practice Examination.
  • You also need a practical experience logbook.
  • The exam is offered twice a year, with application deadlines.
  • Registration is granted by a State or Territory Architects Registration Board, and extra costs apply.

Common reasons AACA applications stall

Six avoidable mistakes, and how to fix each one.

1
Mistake 1 of 6

×Sending documents in stages

The fixLodge only when everything is ready.

AACA needs a complete application at submission and does not accept supporting documents later.

Mistake 2 of 6

×Submitting more than four projects

The fixChoose three or four strong ones.

AACA accepts a maximum of four projects, including one complex project.

Mistake 3 of 6

×Pretty drawings, no competency link

The fixTie every project to the NSCA.

Map each project to topic areas and performance criteria, with supporting drawings or sketches.

Mistake 4 of 6

×Forgetting English evidence

The fixCheck the Applicants Guide early.

Provide an accepted English test, or an Australian architectural employer letter, if your course was not in English.

Mistake 5 of 6

×Expecting a pre-check on your degree

The fixConfirm your pathway first.

AACA cannot judge your qualification until a complete application and fee have been received.

Mistake 6 of 6

×Treating feedback as optional

The fixUse your one feedback round well.

AACA gives one round of feedback by email, then you must revise and resubmit within the time limits.

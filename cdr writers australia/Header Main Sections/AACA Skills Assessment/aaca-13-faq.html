AACA skill assessment FAQs

Answers on the AACA skills assessment, AACA migration skills assessment and the AACA skills assessment fee.

What is the AACA skill assessment?
The AACA skill assessment is run by the Architects Accreditation Council of Australia. It checks whether your architecture qualification and competencies meet Australian standards, and a Suitable outcome can provide a Skilled Migration Assessment for the occupation Architect (OSCA 241131, previously ANZSCO 232111).
How much is the AACA skills assessment fee?
For new Overseas Qualifications Assessment applicants, the fee is $4,973.01 with an Australian card or $5,042.10 with an overseas card. Stage 2 only is $3,044.70 or $3,087.00, and renewal is $446.56 or $452.76. AACA can change fees, so check its fees page before paying.
How long does the AACA migration skills assessment take?
AACA generally finalises a complete application within about 7 weeks of lodgement, or about 12 weeks when documents are incomplete. These times exclude scheduling of the Competency Assessment Interview and can lengthen in peak periods.
Does the AACA skills assessment expire?
Yes. A Skilled Migration Assessment is valid for three years from its issue date. If you previously had a successful combined outcome, you can renew it through the AACA portal, and renewals are issued in approximately 10 working days.
Is the AACA skill assessment the same as architect registration?
No. The assessment can support a migration application and lets you apply for the Architectural Practice Examination. You still need to complete that process and register with the relevant State or Territory Architects Registration Board.
Do I need an OQA if I studied architecture in Australia?
Not if you hold an accredited Master of Architecture from Australia, New Zealand or Hong Kong. You can apply for a Verification of Australian Accredited Architecture Qualification instead.
Can I send missing documents after I apply?
No. AACA requires applications to be complete when submitted, with all supporting documents in the required format.
How many projects should my portfolio include?
Include three to four architectural projects, with one complex project. AACA accepts a maximum of four.

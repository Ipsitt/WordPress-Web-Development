ACECQA skills assessment occupations and ANZSCO codes

Five occupations, each with its own qualification, experience and English rules.

241111

Early Childhood (Pre-Primary School) Teacher

ACECQA-approved early childhood teaching qualification or equivalent. No work experience requirement.

134111

Child Care Centre Manager

Approved diploma or higher, plus 3 years of centre manager experience in a regulated service.

421111

Child Care Worker (group leaders only)

Approved diploma or higher, plus 1 year as a group leader in a regulated service.

421111

Child Care Worker

Approved Certificate III or higher. English evidence at IELTS 5.0 level.

421114

Out of School Hours Care Worker

CHC50221, CHC40221, CHC30221 or 10983NAT, or dual assessment against OSHC standards.

Is your occupation on the list?

Your nominated occupation must be on the Skilled Occupations List with ACECQA named as the assessing authority. Confirm with the Department of Home Affairs that your visa pathway needs an assessment.

ACECQA cannot advise on visa or points questions. Those go to the Department of Home Affairs.

Four ways to show you hold an approved early childhood qualification

ACECQA accepts a qualification in one of four ways. Pick the one that matches your history.

Nationally approved qualification

Your qualification already appears on ACECQA's published list of approved early childhood teaching, diploma or certificate III qualifications. You provide a certified parchment and a full official transcript covering all years of study.

Check the Qualifications CheckerParchmentFull transcript

Taken to hold under former law

People approved before 1 January 2012 (1 August 2012 in WA) who were working in a regulated service can be taken to hold an approved qualification. You add the approval letter and evidence of that earlier Australian employment.

Before 1 Jan 2012WA: 1 Aug 2012Approval letter

Previously assessed by ACECQA

If ACECQA has already assessed and approved your qualification as equivalent, you provide the decision letter or certificate. This route does not need the parchment and transcript again.

Decision letterCertificate

Qualification assessed during your application

If you do not hold an approved qualification, ACECQA assesses it against the National Quality Framework as part of the same skills assessment. There is no separate application and no extra fee, but you need certified colour copies and English evidence.

No extra feeCertified colour copiesEnglish evidence

ACECQA does not accept work experience in place of a formal qualification.

ACECQA work experience rules for centre managers and group leaders

Manager and group leader applications are judged on regulated, post-qualification employment.

01

3 years as a centre manager

Full-time or part-time equivalent, in a regulated early childhood education and care service.

02

1 year as a group leader

Full-time or part-time equivalent, in a regulated service, for the Child Care Worker (group leaders only) route.

03

6 months in the last 3 years

At least six months full-time equivalent must fall within the three years before you apply.

04

After your qualification

Only employment completed after the qualification was awarded counts.

What each employer document must show

Formal employment contractFormal position descriptionEvidence the service was regulatedEmployer Reference templateEmployment Experience template

The Employer Reference must come from your direct supervisor or someone who oversaw or witnessed your work, and contracts and position descriptions are normally issued on letterhead at the start of employment.

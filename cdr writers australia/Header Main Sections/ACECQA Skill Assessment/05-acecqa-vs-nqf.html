ACECQA migration skills assessment vs NQF qualification assessment

Two ACECQA processes with similar names that do different jobs.

≠
Migration skills assessment
  • Used for skilled migration and visa applications.
  • Needs a Skills Assessment Application and the assessment fee.
  • Covers the five listed occupations, with experience rules where they apply.
NQF qualification assessment
  • Recognises overseas qualifications for working in Australian services.
  • Not for migration or visa purposes.
  • An NQF assessment alone does not satisfy a visa requirement.
Good to know: if you hold no approved qualification, the NQF assessment is built into your migration application, so you do not lodge two separate applications.

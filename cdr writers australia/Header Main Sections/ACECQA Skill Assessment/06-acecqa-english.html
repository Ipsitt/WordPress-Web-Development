ACECQA English language requirements by occupation

Required scores depend on the occupation and the qualification route.

OccupationIELTS requirement
Early Childhood TeacherIELTS Academic: 7.0 in reading and writing, 8.0 in speaking and listening
Child Care WorkerIELTS General or Academic: 5.0 in each component
OSHC WorkerIELTS General or Academic: 5.0 in each component
Centre Manager and group leaderNo test evidence listed in the guidelines for the migration route

Other accepted English evidence

01

Study in an exempt country

One year of full-time tertiary study in Australia, NZ, Ireland, Canada, the UK or the USA.

02

ISLPR Level 4

Teacher-focused ISLPR at 4 in all four skills, taken at an approved site within three years.

03

Three-year window

IELTS and ISLPR results must be from the last three years.

04

Employer reference

A professional reference from an employer in an exempt country can be considered case by case.

English evidence is needed where the qualification is assessed under the NQF or the occupation lists a requirement, so check your route before you book a test.

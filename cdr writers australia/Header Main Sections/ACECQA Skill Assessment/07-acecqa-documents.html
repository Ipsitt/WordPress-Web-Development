Certification and translation rules

Small certification slips are one of the easiest ways to delay an application.

  • Certified colour copies of originals, not black and white.
  • Certify the front of every page, never a blank back.
  • Certifier writes their name, position, date and stamp or seal.
  • In Australia, use a person authorised in your state or territory.
  • Overseas, use someone legally authorised in that country.
  • Non-English documents need a certified translation, with the original alongside.
  • Translations in Australia must come from a NAATI-accredited translator.

Scan documents in high resolution colour, with all edges visible, as PDF, JPEG or PNG, and do not share Google Drive or other unsecured links.

Common reasons ACECQA skills assessment applications are delayed

Six mistakes that stall otherwise strong applications.

Mixing up the two assessments

An NQF qualification assessment does not meet the migration requirement.

Counting work in unregulated settings

Experience must be in a regulated early childhood service.

Vague employer references

References should state role, dates, hours and duties, from a supervisor or witness.

Wrong certification

Colour copies need a qualified certifier on the front of each page.

Experience before the qualification

Only employment completed after the award date counts.

Missing payment or forms

Applications are not processed without the fee and signed declarations.

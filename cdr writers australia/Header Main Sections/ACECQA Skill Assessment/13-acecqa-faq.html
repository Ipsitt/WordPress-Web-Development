ACECQA skills assessment FAQs: fees, processing time, English and occupations

Straight answers to what childcare and early childhood educators search before they apply.

How much does an ACECQA skills assessment cost?
The ACECQA skills assessment application fee is A$1,100 including GST, and a review application is A$990. Fees are paid in Australian dollars when you submit, ACECQA indexes them, and they are not refunded to unsuccessful or withdrawn applicants.
How long does an ACECQA skills assessment take?
ACECQA aims to finish within 80 calendar days of receiving an Assessment Ready application. Missing information or documents delay the start of that period, and there is no fast-track option.
How long is an ACECQA skills assessment valid?
A successful ACECQA skills assessment is valid for three years from the issue date. After that you must apply again, and the outcome does not guarantee a visa.
Which occupations does ACECQA assess?
ACECQA assesses Early Childhood (Pre-Primary School) Teacher 241111, Child Care Centre Manager 134111, Child Care Worker (group leaders only) 421111, Child Care Worker 421111 and Out of School Hours Care Worker 421114.
Is the ACECQA migration assessment the same as the NQF qualification assessment?
No. The NQF qualification assessment recognises qualifications for working in Australian services and is not for migration. For a visa you need a skills assessment application, which can include an NQF assessment of your qualification at no extra cost.
Does ACECQA need IELTS for an early childhood teacher skills assessment?
The teacher route asks for IELTS Academic with 7.0 in reading and writing and 8.0 in speaking and listening, within three years. ISLPR Level 4 in all four skills or a year of full-time tertiary study in an exempt country can also be accepted.
How much work experience do I need for an ACECQA assessment?
Child Care Centre Managers need three years and group leaders need one year, full-time equivalent in a regulated service. At least six months must be within the last three years. The teacher, child care worker and OSHC worker routes list no experience requirement.
Who can certify my documents for ACECQA?
In Australia, a person authorised in your state or territory such as a Justice of the Peace, solicitor or police sergeant can certify. Overseas, someone legally authorised in that country can. Certified copies must be in colour, and every page needs certification on the front.
Can I apply for an ACECQA provisional skills assessment?
Yes, if you are an international student graduate with an ACECQA approved diploma from a CRICOS-registered provider and plan to apply for a Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485). The provisional assessment costs A$330 and has an 80-day target.
What happens if my ACECQA skills assessment is unsuccessful?
You receive reasons and information about review. Speak to your case officer first. If evidence was overlooked, ACECQA reopens it free, and if you have new evidence you lodge a Review Application Form with the A$990 fee.

ACS skills assessment for ICT migration: what applicants need to know first

The Australian Computer Society (ACS) is the assessing authority for ICT, data science and cyber security occupations in Australian skilled migration. An ACS skills assessment decides whether your qualifications and work history meet the standard for the ANZSCO occupation you nominate.

Which route you take depends on your background. Some applicants are assessed on their degree, others on their experience through the Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) pathway. Choosing the wrong route is one of the most expensive early mistakes, so the sections below explain how each one works.

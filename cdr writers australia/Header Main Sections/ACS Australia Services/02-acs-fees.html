ACS skills assessment fees: General Skills, Post Australian Study and RPL

These are the application fees ACS lists for each pathway, in Australian dollars.

Pathway or serviceACS feeTypically used for
General Skills$1,498Degree plus work experience

Who you pay

The assessment fee goes to ACS, the assessing authority, when you submit your application online. ACS accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. Any fee a consultancy charges for report writing, review or document preparation is separate and is not paid to ACS.

Fee changes

ACS reserves the right to change its fees at any time, and its fee page does not state GST treatment or an effective date. Check the official page on the day you apply. Appeal fees may be refunded if the appeal succeeds.

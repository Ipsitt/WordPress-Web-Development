ACS RPL Requirements: who qualifies for Recognition of Prior Learning

RPL suits skilled ICT professionals who learned on the job. Check these six rules before you commit.

6 years of ICT work

RPL needs six years of relevant professional experience in your nominated field.

20 hours a week or more

Only paid professional work of at least 20 hours per week counts toward that total.

Recent experience

Your most recent role must be current, or have ended within two years of your application.

From age 18

Only professional ICT work after your 18th birthday is counted.

Paid work only

ACS does not count unpaid internships, volunteering, or research done only to meet a thesis requirement.

Duties over job title

ACS focuses on the duties you performed and the depth of complexity, and your pay should reflect the skill level.

Does your background point to RPL? ACS describes RPL as the pathway for people with ample ICT, data science or cyber security experience who lack the relevant tertiary qualifications, and it does not assess any qualification. If you hold a relevant ICT degree, General Skills or Post Australian Study is usually the better fit. ACS gives no pre-assessment advice, so choose carefully.

ACS RPL application checklist: documents you must submit

Missing evidence is a common reason ACS sends a request for more information, which adds weeks to your timeline.

  • Proof of identityTwo forms of photo ID, plus proof of any name change.
  • Two RPL project reportsBoth reports go inside the ACS RPL Form.
  • Professional currency evidenceAt least two forms that show your skills are current for the ANZSCO code you nominated.
  • Employment evidenceEmployer documents showing your employment periods, plus pay evidence that matches the skill level.
  • Vendor certifications (optional)Available for DevOps and Cyber Security applicants only.
Applications must be completed within 90 days of starting them in the ACS portal, so gather your employer letters and payslips before you begin.

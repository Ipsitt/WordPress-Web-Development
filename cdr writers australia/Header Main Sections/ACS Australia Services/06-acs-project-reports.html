Map to the Core Body of Knowledge

ACS directs applicants to the ACS Core Body of Knowledge (CBOK) for guidance on what the reports should demonstrate. Choose projects that show the depth and breadth of ICT knowledge you built in your career, then check the current RPL Form.

Show depth, not a job description

ACS wants a specific episode: your role, the problem, the technologies, your decisions and the result. Applicants in non-project roles describe their work and the challenges they faced in that role instead.

Use the current ACS RPL Form for the exact structure, since ACS updates it from time to time.

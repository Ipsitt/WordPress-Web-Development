ACS plagiarism and originality rules for RPL project reports

Know the rules before anything is written. They protect you from a failed or cancelled assessment.

Your own work

ACS states that reports must be entirely your own work and must not be outsourced to a third party, such as a writing or editing agency.

Similarity checks

ACS may use software to compare your reports with published material and with other applications, and it can ask you to resubmit for plagiarism screening.

Reference and declare

Reference any quoted or paraphrased material, and declare anything you did not create yourself. Fabricated or borrowed projects put your whole application at risk.

How we support you within these rules

Our role is guidance: explaining what ACS expects, helping you pick the strongest projects, structuring your material against the Core Body of Knowledge, and reviewing your own draft for clarity and gaps. The technical detail and the final text must come from you and reflect work you actually did.

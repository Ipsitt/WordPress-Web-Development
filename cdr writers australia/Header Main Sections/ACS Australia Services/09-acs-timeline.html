ACS skills assessment processing time, priority processing and outcome validity

Plan your visa dates around these official timeframes.

Submit a complete application

Include every document in the first submission. Requests for extra information pause the clock and extend the wait.

Standard processing

ACS asks applicants to allow 4 to 6 weeks when no further documents or information are needed.

Priority processing

If your visa deadline is under 12 weeks from your application date, you can request priority handling. ACS asks for at least two weeks' notice and lists no fee for it.

Receive your outcome

Suitable outcomes are valid for two years and arrive as a PDF you can use as evidence with the Department of Home Affairs.

Common ACS RPL mistakes that delay or sink a skills assessment

Avoid these six problems and you remove most of the risk from your application.

Choosing the wrong pathway

RPL is for applicants without a relevant tertiary qualification. A graduate on the wrong pathway can pay for an assessment that does not fit their evidence.

Wrong ANZSCO code

Picking a code from a job title instead of the duties performed leads to a mismatch with your evidence.

Counting part-time or unpaid work

Work under 20 hours a week, or unpaid, is not counted toward your six years.

Thin employment evidence

Employer letters without dates, duties or matching pay evidence invite requests for more information.

Projects outside the windows

A project older than the two-year and four-year windows does not meet the RPL recency rule.

Generic, copied text

Text borrowed from other reports or templates can trigger similarity flags and a resubmission request.

ACS RPL and skills assessment FAQs

Straight answers to questions ICT applicants ask before they apply.

Who do I pay the ACS skills assessment fee to?
You pay ACS directly through its online application portal, by credit card or PayPal. Fees for preparation or review services from a consultancy are separate and not paid to ACS.
How many years of experience do I need for ACS RPL?
ACS requires six years of relevant professional ICT work, counting only roles of at least 20 hours a week, and your most recent experience must be current or have ended within the last two years.
Do internships and volunteering count for ACS RPL?
No. ACS states that unpaid internships and volunteer services are not considered paid employment, and research done only to meet a thesis requirement is not work experience. Only paid professional ICT work of at least 20 hours a week counts.
Does my job title decide my ANZSCO occupation?
No. ACS compares your actual duties with the occupation description, so choose the code that matches the work you really do.
What is the difference between ICT major and minor?
These labels describe how much ICT content ACS finds in your degree, and they set how much work experience you need. For a closely related major under General Skills, ACS lists 2 years in the last 10 or 4 years overall. For a minor it lists 5 years in the last 10 or 6 years overall. ACS decides the label at formal assessment.
Can ACS tell if my report was written by someone else?
ACS says reports must be your own work and it may use similarity software. It can also ask you to resubmit for plagiarism screening, so every report should reflect your own projects.
Does the ACS Professional Year replace the skills assessment?
No. You still need an assessment. Under the Post Australian Study pathway, though, completing the ACS Professional Year can stand in for the one year of post-degree work experience.
Can I get priority processing for a visa deadline?
If your visa deadline is under 12 weeks away, ACS may prioritise your application. You must give at least two weeks' notice, and ACS lists no fee for this.

ACS, also known as the Australian Computer Society, is responsible for the information and communications technology professionals.

RPL report is written to the Australian Computer Society for those who are willing to migrate to Australia at the outset of an ICT-related career but do not hold a degree of ICT qualification or come from a background rather than IT. ACS RPL report format

There are two sections in this format which are explained below. In this format applicants should demonstrate their professional skills.

Section 1: The key area of knowledge
  • Essential ICT Knowledge
  • General ICT Knowledge
Section 2: The RPL Project Report

In this section, you have to provide the details of the projects chosen. For this, you need to provide two project reports: one must be prepared based on a project you undertook within the last three years and another for a project within the last five years.

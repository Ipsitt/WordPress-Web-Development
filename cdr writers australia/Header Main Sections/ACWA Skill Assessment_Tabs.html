ACWA Application Format & Requirements

The ACWA assessment process generally involves reviewing:

Eligibility Checklist

  • Relevant community work or social welfare qualification
  • Core units and competency coverage
  • Qualification comparability to Australian standards
  • Employment relevance (if required)
  • Proper documentation format
We provide guidance to ensure your documentation aligns with ACWA assessment requirements.

Key Documents Typically Required

  • Academic qualification certificates
  • Academic transcript
  • Detailed course curriculum / syllabus
  • Employment reference letters (if applicable)
  • Resume/CV
  • Identity documents

What is ACWA Skill Assessment?

Australian Community Workers Association (ACWA) is an Australian assessing authority responsible for assessing community work and social welfare qualifications for migration purposes. An ACWA skill assessment verifies whether your qualifications and, where applicable, employment experience meet Australian standards for community work and related occupations.

Now Community Work Australia

The assessing authority formerly known as ACWA now operates as Community Work Australia (CWA).

Three criteria

CWA checks your qualification, your English language proficiency, and your employment and industry currency.

One occupation per application

You nominate a single occupation, and each extra occupation needs its own application and full fee.

Portal only

CWA states it does not accept skills assessment applications by email.

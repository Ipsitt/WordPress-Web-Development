Occupations covered by an ACWA skills assessment, with ANZSCO codes

Community Work Australia assesses these welfare support occupations. The pathway shows which visa program each one can use.

OccupationANZSCO codeAssessment pathway

Family Support Worker411713DAMA (Western Australia)
Parole or Probation Officer411714DAMA only
Residential Care Officer411715DAMA (Western Australia)

Also assessed for labour agreements

CWA also assesses Welfare Worker, Refuge Worker, Aged or Disabled Carer and Child or Youth Residential Care Assistant for labour agreement programs.

Social Workers go elsewhere

Social Worker is not on CWA's list of assessed occupations. Social Workers apply to the Australian Association of Social Workers (AASW).

Check the visa list yourself

Which visas an occupation can support changes, so check the Department of Home Affairs skilled occupation list before you lodge.

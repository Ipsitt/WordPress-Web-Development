ACWA qualification requirements: three routes at AQF level 5 or higher

Your qualification decides how much employment evidence you need. Hover over a card to see its experience rule.

Route 1

CWA accredited diploma or higher

A diploma or higher accredited by Community Work Australia. Field placements in the course are treated as meeting the industry experience requirement.

Experience: Because the placement counts as experience, no separate employment evidence is needed for this part of the assessment.

Route 2

Recognised relevant qualification

A bachelor's degree or higher with a community and human services focus, professionally accredited by a body whose standards match CWA's.

Experience: Field placement or practicum is treated as sufficient industry experience, as with an accredited qualification.

Route 3

Relevant diploma or higher

A diploma or higher related to community services that is not accredited or professionally recognised.

Experience: You must add post-qualification industry experience, with the required length depending on your occupation.

RPL is capped at 40%

Recognition of prior learning can count for no more than 40% of your qualification, and CWA may reject RPL based on unsuitable experience or study.

Graduate certificates

A graduate certificate or graduate diploma must follow a relevant undergraduate or related qualification.

Unduly short courses

CWA does not accept qualifications it considers unduly short for the occupation.

Youth Worker detail

For Youth Worker, a recognised youth work qualification needs at least 400 hours of fieldwork in a youth setting.

ACWA English language requirements for tests taken on or after 7 August 2025

You can meet the English criterion in one of three ways. Employment is no longer one of them.

1. A recent test result

Use a test taken within three years of your application, at the required scores. Multiple IELTS or MET results are allowed if all were taken within 12 months.

2. Study in English

Complete a bachelor's, master's or doctorate, or all secondary schooling plus a diploma or higher, delivered and assessed in English in Australia, Canada, Ireland, New Zealand, the UK or the USA.

3. A prior CWA outcome

Reuse an earlier suitable CWA outcome that is still valid, or that expired no more than six months before your new application.

Not accepted

CWA does not accept CELPIP Online, IELTS Online, LANGUAGECERT Academic Online, MET Digital (at-home) or TOEFL iBT Home Edition, and it does not accept combined results from test types other than IELTS.

Older tests

Tests taken on or before 6 August 2025 stay valid for up to three years from the test date, under the earlier score table. Always confirm scores on CWA's occupation page before booking a test.

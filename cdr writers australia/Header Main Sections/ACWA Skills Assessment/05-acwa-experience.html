ACWA work experience and industry currency requirements by occupation

Experience must be paid direct service work, completed after your qualification and within the last four years. Full-time means 30 hours or more per week.

411711ANZSCO code
1 yearexperience with a relevant diploma
4 yearswindow for that experience
AQF 5skill level of the role
  • With a CWA accredited or recognised qualification, field placements are treated as meeting the experience requirement.
  • With a relevant diploma or higher, you need at least one year of full-time (or part-time equivalent) industry experience within the last four years.
  • Roles that only need an AQF level 3 or 4 certificate do not count.

  • Employment must be paid, direct service to people with disability, post-qualification and at AQF level 5 skill level.
  • A relevant specialised diploma or higher needs at least 12 months full-time (or equivalent).
  • A CWA accredited specialised diploma or higher is deemed to meet the requirement through field placement.
  • A non-specialised relevant community services qualification needs at least two years full-time (or equivalent).

  • Accredited or recognised youth work qualifications are deemed to meet the experience requirement through practicum.
  • A specialised youth work diploma needs at least one year of full-time youth services employment at the required skill level.
  • A non-specialised qualification at AQF level 5 or higher needs two years.

Industry currency: meet any one of these

Qualification under 4 years old

Your qualification was completed less than four years ago.

3 months of current work

At least three months of full-time (or equivalent) relevant employment in a current role at the required skill level.

6 months in 18 months

At least six months of relevant employment at the required skill level within the last 18 months.

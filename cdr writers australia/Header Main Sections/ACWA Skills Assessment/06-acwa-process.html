ACWA Skills Assessment Application Process: Six Steps on the CWA Portal

Applications are portal only. CWA does not accept them by email.

1

Match your occupation

Check the occupations CWA assesses and find the closest match to your qualification and experience.

2

Nominate one occupation

Read the ANZSCO description and confirm you meet the criteria. Only one occupation is allowed per application.

3

Prepare your documents

Collect everything on CWA's document checklist before you start.

4

Create a portal account

Apply online, track your status and download your outcome letter from the skills assessment portal.

5

Submit in one session

Choose your migration program (general skilled, WA DAMA or ACILA) and upload your evidence. Drafts may lose attached files if you exit.

6

Wait for your outcome

An assessment officer reviews your file. Your status moves from Submitted to In Progress and finally Suitable or Not Suitable.

Stuck on a step?

Talk to us before you lodge and we will help you check your evidence against the criteria.

Know the status labels

You will see Draft, Submitted, In Progress, Documents Requested, Not Suitable or Suitable. CWA asks you not to contact the office while your status still reads Submitted, because processing happens within the allotted timeframe.

Refunds before work starts

A refund may be considered while your status is still Submitted. If approved, CWA keeps an AUD 80 service fee and refunds the rest within four weeks.

No refund once work begins

If the status has moved on, or a document check has begun, no refund is issued.

Withdraw by email

Withdrawals and refund requests go to CWA by email, because the portal does not let you withdraw manually.

CWA's fee page shows no effective date, so confirm the current amount before you pay.

No paid fast-track

CWA offers no paid priority service. After your application is formally submitted, you can email a priority request with documentary evidence. Requests are assessed case by case and are not guaranteed.

What causes delays

Missing documents, trouble verifying documents, difficulty contacting referees or employers, incomplete applications and outdated contact details.

CWA calls these timeframes approximate. Its processing times page was last updated on 22 November 2025.

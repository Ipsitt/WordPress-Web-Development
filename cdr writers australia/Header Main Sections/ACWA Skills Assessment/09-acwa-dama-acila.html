ACWA DAMA and ACILA skills assessments: labour agreement pathways

Employer-sponsored programs use separate pathways, with different fees, timeframes and experience rules.

DAMA (Western Australia)

CWA is the approved assessing authority for Western Australia's DAMA occupations. You first need employment, or an offer, with an employer that has a labour agreement. The fee is AUD 965 and the target is 6 to 8 weeks.

ACILA (aged care)

The Aged Care Industry Labour Agreement pathway covers Aged or Disabled Carer. The fee is AUD 595 and the target is 4 to 6 weeks.

Extra experience for WA DAMA, by visa program

Skills in Demand (SID)1 year of additional experience
SESR and ENS, regional Western Australia2 years of additional experience
SESR and ENS, metropolitan Perth3 years of additional experience

Experience is counted at 30 hours a week or the part-time equivalent. Applicants for other DAMA regions should contact that region's assessing authority.

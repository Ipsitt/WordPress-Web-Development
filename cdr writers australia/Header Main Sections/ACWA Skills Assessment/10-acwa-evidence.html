ACWA evidence rules: what makes an application decision-ready

These rules come from CWA's own application guidance.

  • Evidence for every claimCWA cannot accept claims without sufficient evidence. Contracts, variations, job offers and job ads do not replace required evidence, though they can support it.
  • If your employer can't helpA statutory declaration containing all the required information may be accepted, but claims must be verifiable. If your employer has stopped trading, email CWA for advice.
  • Master's degree holdersYou must also provide the qualifications underpinning your master's degree, such as your bachelor's degree, including non-relevant ones.
  • Decision-ready on lodgingApplications must be complete when lodged. You can email extra documents with your Application ID, but not once the status is In Progress.
  • Using a migration agentAgents need their own portal login and must include a completed, signed Agent Authorisation Form. Changing agents needs Form 956 or the Agent Authorisation Form with the revoke box ticked.
  • Changing your occupationYou can change your nominated occupation or add claims by email only before the status reaches In Progress. After that it is at the assessment officer's discretion.
Tip: CWA says drafts can lose attached documents if you exit the portal, so gather everything first and submit in one session.

ACWA skills assessment FAQs: fees, processing time, English and occupations

Straight answers to what community services professionals search before they apply.

What is the ACWA skills assessment?
It is the migration skills assessment for community services occupations, run by Community Work Australia (CWA), the body formerly known as the Australian Community Workers Association (ACWA). It checks your qualification, English language proficiency, and employment and industry currency.
Which occupations does ACWA assess?
The general skilled pathway covers Community Worker (ANZSCO 411711), Disability Services Officer (411712) and Youth Worker (411716). CWA also assesses Family Support Worker, Parole or Probation Officer and Residential Care Officer for DAMA, plus other labour agreement occupations such as Aged or Disabled Carer.
How much does an ACWA skills assessment cost?
The general skilled and DAMA assessments each cost AUD 965 including GST, and the aged care (ACILA) assessment costs AUD 595. An internal review is AUD 500 and an external appeal is AUD 750. Confirm current fees on CWA's site before you pay.
How long does an ACWA skills assessment take?
CWA's approximate targets are 10 to 12 weeks for general skilled occupations, 6 to 8 weeks for DAMA and 4 to 6 weeks for aged care. There is no paid fast-track, and missing documents or hard-to-verify evidence can extend the wait.
How long is an ACWA skills assessment valid?
A Community Work Australia outcome is valid for three years from the issue date on the outcome letter. If it expires, you need a new application under the current guidelines.
What are the ACWA English language requirements?
You can use a recent test (within three years), study in English in Australia, Canada, Ireland, New Zealand, the UK or the USA, or a suitable earlier CWA outcome. For tests on or after 7 August 2025, IELTS needs 7 in each band. Employment can no longer be used as English evidence.
What qualification do I need for an ACWA assessment?
You need an AQF level 5 diploma or higher that is CWA accredited, a recognised relevant bachelor's degree or higher, or a relevant community services diploma or higher. RPL is capped at 40%, and graduate certificates must follow a relevant undergraduate qualification.
How much work experience does ACWA require?
It depends on your qualification and occupation. A relevant diploma holder in Community Worker needs one year full-time within the last four years, while a non-specialised qualification for Disability Services Officer or Youth Worker needs two years. Accredited or recognised qualifications are deemed to meet the requirement through field placements.
What is industry currency for ACWA?
You meet currency with any one of these: a qualification less than four years old, at least three months of full-time relevant current employment, or at least six months of relevant employment within the last 18 months.
What is the ANZSCO code for a Youth Worker?
The ANZSCO code for Youth Worker is 411716. Community Worker is 411711 and Disability Services Officer is 411712.
Can Social Workers use the ACWA skills assessment?
No. Social Worker is not an occupation CWA assesses. Social Workers apply to the Australian Association of Social Workers (AASW).
Can I appeal an ACWA skills assessment outcome?
Yes. If your outcome is not suitable, you can request a review with new evidence, or appeal if you believe the decision was wrong on the original evidence. Both must be requested by email within 28 days of receiving the outcome, and fees apply.

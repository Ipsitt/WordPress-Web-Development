Occupations covered by an AIMS skills assessment, with ANZSCO codes

AIMS assesses these four laboratory occupations. Each has its own guidelines and its own application form.

MLS also covers MLT

AIMS states that applicants found suitable as Medical Laboratory Scientists are also deemed suitable as Medical Laboratory Technicians.

AIMS Assessment Pathways by Occupation

Choose an occupation to see its qualification, experience and exam rules.

Option 1

AIMS accredited degree

Graduates of an AIMS accredited degree are classified as MLS without sitting the professional examination, and postgraduate experience is not required. You must confirm your subjects meet AIMS's accredited subject pathway.

Experience: Best case: no exam and no experience requirement, but your subjects must match.

Option 2

Acceptable science degree

Stage 1 is a document assessment and Stage 2 is the AIMS MLS Professional Examination. The degree must be AQF level 7, 8 or 9, or a combination.

Experience: Needs at least two years full-time (or part-time equivalent) postgraduate experience in a medical pathology laboratory, with one year in the five years before you apply.

Option 3

Postgraduate study plus laboratory experience

Stage 1 is a document assessment and Stage 2 is the AIMS MLS Special Professional Examination. You need a science degree plus a Master's with a pathology laboratory research project, a professional doctorate or a PhD.

Experience: Needs two years of relevant post-doctoral work in a medical pathology laboratory, one year within the last five. Research-only employment is not looked on favourably.

What counts as experience: AIMS only counts experience gained in a recognised medical pathology laboratory after you complete your degree or diploma. Medical graduates have separate requirements in the guidelines. Hover over a card to see its experience rule.

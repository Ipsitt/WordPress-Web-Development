  • Option 1: a medical laboratory science qualification assessed as comparable to an Australian diploma (at least AQF level 5), with relevant pathology subjects.
  • Option 1 also needs two years full-time (or part-time equivalent) diagnostic pathology experience within the five years before you apply.
  • Option 2: current full (not provisional) registration with the Medical Sciences Council of New Zealand as a Medical Laboratory Technician.

  • All three criteria apply: a pathology practice award comparable to the Australian HLT37215 Certificate III, two years full-time (or equivalent) experience within five years, and a current first aid certificate equivalent to HLTAID010.
  • Experience counts only if it was gained in a recognised medical pathology laboratory after your qualification.
  • AIMS also assesses relevant experience from the last ten years, which can matter for visa points.
  • The Pathology Collector exam is a 1.5-hour multiple-choice paper, and you need 50% or more to pass.

  • IVF Embryologist is a specialisation of Medical Laboratory Scientist, with its own AIMS guidelines and application.
  • If you hold a specialised postgraduate degree in clinical embryology or reproductive biology, your institution must complete AIMS's Course Information Form.
  • Only applicants assessed as eligible receive a Professional Examination Invitation Letter and application form.
  • AIMS says it issues your lodgement reference number and fee invoice within ten business days. Confirm the experience rules in the current IVF guidelines.

AIMS skills assessment process: Seven Steps from Occupation to Outcome

The order below follows AIMS's own application guidance. Your occupation's guidelines set the detail.

1

Choose your occupation

Match your qualifications and laboratory duties to one of the four ANZSCO codes.

2

Read the guidelines

Use the occupation-specific guidelines and the checklist built into the application form.

3

Gather and verify evidence

Collect identity, education, employment and English evidence, and arrange transcripts to reach AIMS correctly.

4

Lodge your application

Submit the form and documents. AIMS says it issues a lodgement reference number and fee invoice within ten business days.

5

Pay and wait for verification

AIMS checks your identity, English, employment, education and any registration. Missing items extend the wait.

6

Sit the exam, if invited

Only applicants found eligible receive an exam invitation. Others receive their assessment outcome without an exam.

7

Receive your outcome

AIMS decides suitable or not suitable for each nominated occupation and sends a results letter.

Stage 1 and Stage 2

For exam routes, AIMS runs a Stage 1 document assessment first. You can only register for the Stage 2 professional examination once Stage 1 shows you are eligible.

AIMS English language requirements

English evidence is part of every AIMS file, but the score rules moved to the Department of Home Affairs.

Scores no longer in AIMS forms

From 12 March 2026, AIMS removed the required test scores from its guidelines and forms. Check the Department of Home Affairs website for the current required scores.

No One Skill Retake

AIMS does not accept English results that include a One Skill Retake component.

One sitting, one report

Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking must be completed in one sitting, with the required scores in the same result. AIMS accepts a single test report only.

Accepted tests

AIMS's MLS and MLT guidelines list IELTS (Academic or General Training), TOEFL, PTE Academic (submitted online to AIMS), OET in certain professions, and Cambridge C1 Advanced. Confirm the list in your occupation's current guidelines.

Validity

A test report is valid if AIMS receives it with your application within three years of the test date, and there are no exemptions from providing one.

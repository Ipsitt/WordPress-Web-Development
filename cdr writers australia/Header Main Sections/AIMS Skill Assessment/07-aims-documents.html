AIMS document specifications and how AIMS verifies them

Small formatting slips cause delays, so check each detail before you upload.

  • IdentityA colour scan of your passport bio-data page, one recent colour photo taken within six months against a light plain background (self-taken photos are not accepted), your birth certificate, and at least one secondary ID.
  • EducationYour certificate or statement of completion and a complete official transcript showing full subject names. Non-accredited degrees need unit descriptions, and research degrees need a thesis abstract.
  • EmploymentA signed, dated letter on company letterhead from a supervisor, line manager or HR officer with duties and exact start and finish dates, plus payment evidence such as payslips, tax records or bank statements.
  • TranslationsSend a colour scan of the original and a certified translation. Translations you prepare yourself are not accepted.
  • File rulesScan in colour at 600 dpi or better, and keep total attachments under 20MB.
  • RegistrationProvide your professional registration or licence if one is required where you work, plus any membership certificates.

How each document is verified

Australian university transcripts

Shared through My eQuals, or sent as a cryptographically signed PDF.

Australian VET transcripts

Verified through the USI system.

Overseas institutions

Sent directly to AIMS in a sealed envelope, signed and stamped across the back flap.

Employment letters

AIMS contacts the person who signed the letter to confirm it is genuine.

Fraud has a two-year penalty

AIMS states that suspected fraudulent documents lead to rejection and a two-year ban from AIMS applications, so never alter or invent evidence.

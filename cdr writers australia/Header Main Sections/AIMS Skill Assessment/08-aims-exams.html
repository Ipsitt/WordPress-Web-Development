Special Professional Exam

For Option 3 applicants. It is an essay and short-answer paper of two hours with a 50% pass mark, covering topics such as laboratory accreditation, quality control, regulations and ethics.

IVF and collector exams

The IVF Embryologist exam has 80 multiple-choice questions over two hours. The Pathology Collector paper is 1.5 hours with a 50% pass mark.

Enrolment timing

AIMS notifies enrolled candidates of their exam date by the second week of February or August. Contact AIMS if you have heard nothing by 15 February or 15 August.

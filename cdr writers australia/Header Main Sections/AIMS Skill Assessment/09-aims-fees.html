Non-refundable once started

AIMS states that assessment fees are non-refundable once processing has begun.

12-month limit

Applications left incomplete for more than 12 months may be withdrawn and closed by AIMS.

Fees can change

AIMS says fees may change without notice, and its fee page shows no effective date, so confirm the amount before you pay.

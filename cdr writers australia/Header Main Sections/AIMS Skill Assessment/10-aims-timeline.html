AIMS processing time, validity, review and appeal deadlines

Know the clocks before you lodge. Hover over a card.

16 weeks or 6 months

AIMS's website aims to finish assessments within 16 weeks of receipt, while its occupation guidelines state six months. Document verification can take up to six months, so plan for the longer figure.

No express service

AIMS states that it does not offer an express or priority service.

Pending for up to a year

Applications with unresolved issues may be held as Pending for up to one year from receipt.

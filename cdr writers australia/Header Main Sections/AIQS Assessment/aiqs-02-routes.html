The five AIQS assessment routes

Your degree and membership decide the route. Scroll through all five before you choose.

Qualifications OnlyDegree recognition
  • QualificationAIQS-accredited Australian Bachelor’s or postgraduate degree
  • ExperienceNot assessed
  • Good to knowRecognises your degree only. It cannot support a permanent residency application on its own.
Pathway 1Accredited Australian degree
  • QualificationAIQS-accredited degree obtained in Australia
  • ExperienceAt least 1 year full-time, after your degree
  • Good to knowOverseas degrees never qualify here, even if listed in the Accredited Program Guide.
Pathway 2Non-accredited Australian degree
  • QualificationQuantity surveying degree from a CRICOS-recognised Australian course
  • ExperienceAt least 2 years full-time, after your degree
  • Good to knowAIQS does not assess this route with only one year of experience.
Pathway 3Overseas-qualified
  • QualificationApproved overseas degree comparable to AQF Level 7 in Quantity Surveying or Construction Management and Economics
  • ExperienceAt least 2 years full-time, after your degree
  • Good to knowNeeds an approved English test unless exempt. One year of experience is not assessed.
Pathway 4Voting member
  • QualificationBachelor’s degree plus current financial Voting membership of AIQS or a recognised body
  • ExperienceAt least 12 months full-time, after joining
  • Good to knowCPD-compliant members only. You still lodge a full application with all documents.

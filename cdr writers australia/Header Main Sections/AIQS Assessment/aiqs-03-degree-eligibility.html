Which degrees does AIQS accept?

AIQS checks both the level and the subject of your qualification.

Accepted

A degree in Quantity Surveying or Construction Management and Economics, at Bachelor’s level equivalent to AQF Level 7 or higher. A relevant postgraduate qualification can also count.

May be considered, not guaranteed

Degrees in allied disciplines, such as Civil Engineering, can be reviewed. AIQS assesses the course content, so the result is never assured.

Not considered

General engineering, mechanical, electrical and software degrees are not treated as allied disciplines.

You must have graduated

AIQS will not assess you while you are still studying.

No work-only assessments

Your qualification must be suitable before any experience is assessed.

Masters rule

If your first degree is not recognised, only experience after your masters degree may count.

Overseas degrees

Even a degree listed in the Accredited Program Guide must go through Pathway 3 or 4.

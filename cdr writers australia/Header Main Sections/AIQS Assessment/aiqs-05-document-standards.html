Document standards AIQS checks before assessing

Many applications stall on formatting, not on the facts.

Certified colour copies

An authorised person, such as a Justice of the Peace, legal practitioner or police officer, certifies each page. Certification must be dated within 12 months of submission, and every file is uploaded as a PDF.

Non-English documents

Provide certified colour copies of both the original and an English translation by a NAATI-authorised translator.

Course content is checked

Transcripts must be on university letterhead and show subjects, semester, course and grade for every year, including any prior learning. Australian transcripts and testamurs must be certified online through My eQuals.

Passport-size photo

A clear colour photo taken within the last four weeks, with a signed declaration that it is a true likeness and the date.

Passport plus two more

A certified colour bio page, plus at least two of: birth certificate, Australian visa or ImmiCard, another official photo ID, or an ID card.

Keep your originals

AIQS cannot return originals, so send copies only.

Pathways 3 and 4 need an approved English test, unless you are exempt.

Test rules

Any Department of Home Affairs-approved test is accepted, scored at IELTS band 6 or equivalent in each component. Take it within two years of applying, and in one sitting, because AIQS rejects combined results.

Exemptions

Passport holders from Australia, Canada, New Zealand, Ireland, the UK or the USA, and applicants with an Australian Bachelor’s degree or an Australian postgraduate quantity surveying qualification. AIQS may still ask for a test.

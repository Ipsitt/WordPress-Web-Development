  • Company letterheadUse company letterhead. A supervisor who has moved may use their new letterhead and name the company where you worked together.
  • Date signedInclude the date the letter was signed.
  • Role and main dutiesDescribe your role and the main duties you performed.
  • Start and end datesState the exact commencement and end dates of your role.
  • Referrer contact detailsGive the referrer’s contact details.
  • Qualifications and postnominalsThe signer’s qualifications, such as BSc QS or MRICS, with colour copies of each.
  • Written uniquelyEach letter must be written individually, with nothing copied from other letters.

Who can sign

A direct supervisor who is a practising quantity surveyor and either holds a Bachelor’s degree in quantity surveying at AQF Level 7 or higher, or is a financial Voting member of AIQS or a recognised body. HR and professional managers do not qualify.

What the evidence must show

Skills across at least two of the three core competencies and one specialist competency in the AIQS Competency Standards for Quantity Surveyors, Construction Economists and Cost Engineers.

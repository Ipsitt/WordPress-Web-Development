The AIQS application process, step by step

Seven steps from checking your visa to receiving your outcome letter.

1

Check your visa needs

Confirm with Home Affairs that your visa requires an AIQS assessment. AIQS cannot give visa advice.

2

Choose your route

Match your degree and membership to Qualifications Only or Pathways 1 to 4.

3

Gather certified copies

Prepare colour PDFs, certified within the last 12 months.

4

Apply and pay

Complete the application form and pay the fee in Australian dollars.

5

Documentation review

AIQS checks compliance first, which can take up to three months. Deficiencies are emailed to you.

6

Assessment

Your file goes to assessors, who aim to finish within two months of receiving it.

7

Outcome letter

You receive a suitable or not suitable result. There is no partial outcome.

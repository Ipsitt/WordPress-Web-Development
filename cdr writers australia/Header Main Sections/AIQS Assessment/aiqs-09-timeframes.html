How long does the AIQS skills assessment take?

AIQS says migration skills assessments may take up to five months in total.

Documentation review
Up to 3 months
Time to fix deficiencies
2 months, then suspended
Assessor review
Aim: 2 months, complex files longer
Overall AIQS guidance
Up to 5 months
012345 months

The assessor clock starts only once your file passes the documentation review. If you miss the two-month window to supply documents, your application is suspended. AIQS cannot take phone calls on skilled migration, so questions go by email.

After your AIQS outcome: validity, appeals and reassessment

Three dates to put in your calendar the day your letter arrives.

Outcome letter3 months24 months

Validity: 2 years

An assessment is valid for two years from the date on the letter. A later letter voids the earlier one and restarts the clock.

Appeal: 3 months

Appeal in writing within three calendar months of a not suitable letter. The AUD 400 fee (ex GST) is non-refundable, and a panel of two assessors reviews it. The result is final.

Reassessment: 2 years

Lodge a complete new application with an up-to-date file and your outcome letter within 24 months to pay the AUD 350 reassessment fee (ex GST). Later, or after a not suitable result, the full fee applies.

Withdrawing early

You can withdraw by email before your file goes to assessors. A refund is reduced by an AUD 110 admin fee, and none is possible afterwards.

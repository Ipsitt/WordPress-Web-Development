Red flags that sink AIQS applications

Tick anything that sounds like your application and see how to fix it.

  • Use a direct supervisor who is a practising quantity surveyor and meets AIQS’s qualification or membership rule.
  • Each letter must be written uniquely by the supervisor who signs it.
  • Check the allied-discipline rules. General engineering degrees are not considered.
  • Only employment after your qualification, or after your membership for Pathway 4, counts.
  • Have documents re-certified so they are dated within 12 months of submission.
  • AIQS accepts results from a single sitting only.
  • Send only what the checklist email requests, because irrelevant documents can void the file.
  • Experience gained more than ten years before your application is not assessed.
  • AIQS does not assess experience on its own. Your qualification must be suitable first.

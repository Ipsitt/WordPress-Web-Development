How we support your AIQS skills assessment

Practical guidance from first check to outcome letter. You stay in control of your own application.

01

Competency mapping

We match your projects to the AIQS Competency Standards so your evidence covers what assessors expect.

02

Referee briefing

We explain AIQS’s reference letter rules to your supervisors. The letter stays their own work and carries their signature.

03

Route and code check

We confirm your route and your occupation code before you spend on an application.

04

Document review

We check certification dates, transcripts, translations and the checklist before you lodge.

05

Application support

We guide you through the application form, payment and portal upload.

06

Follow-up planning

If AIQS emails about deficiencies, we help you respond within the two-month window.

We offer guidance and document preparation only. AIQS makes every decision, and neither AIQS nor we can give visa advice. We are independent and not affiliated with AIQS, and we cannot promise an outcome.

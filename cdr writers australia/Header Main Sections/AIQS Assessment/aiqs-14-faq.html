AIQS skills assessment FAQs

Corrected answers on pathways, timing, validity and fees.

What is the AIQS skills assessment?
It is the migration skills assessment run by the Australian Institute of Quantity Surveyors for the occupation Quantity Surveyor (OSCA 241932, previously ANZSCO 233213). AIQS checks your qualification first, then your skilled employment, and issues a suitable or not suitable outcome.
Is AIQS the authority for quantity surveyor skills assessments?
Yes. The Department of Home Affairs nominates AIQS as the assessing authority for Quantity Surveyor. AIQS cannot give visa advice, so check your visa requirements with Home Affairs.
How long does an AIQS skills assessment take?
AIQS says migration skills assessments may take up to five months. The documentation review can take up to three months, and assessors then aim to finish within two months, with complex files taking longer.
How long is an AIQS assessment valid?
Two years from the date on the assessment letter. A later letter makes the earlier one void and restarts the validity period.
Can I apply with a civil engineering degree?
AIQS may consider degrees in allied disciplines such as Civil Engineering, but it is not guaranteed. General engineering, mechanical, electrical and software degrees are not considered allied.
Do I need an English test for AIQS?
Applicants on Pathways 3 and 4 need a Department of Home Affairs-approved test at IELTS band 6 or equivalent in each component, unless exempt. Results from different sittings cannot be combined.
Does an AIQS assessment make me an AIQS member?
No. A migration skills assessment is separate from AIQS membership, which needs its own application.
Can I get an assessment based on work experience alone?
No. Your qualification must be assessed as suitable before any work experience is assessed.
Who can write my AIQS reference letter?
A direct supervisor who is a practising quantity surveyor and meets AIQS’s degree or membership rule. HR and professional managers cannot, and each letter must be written uniquely.
How much does the AIQS skills assessment fee cost?
AIQS lists AUD 555 for Qualifications Only, AUD 655 for Pathways 1 to 4, AUD 350 for reassessment and AUD 400 for appeals, all excluding GST. Fees are in Australian dollars, and a credit card surcharge applies.

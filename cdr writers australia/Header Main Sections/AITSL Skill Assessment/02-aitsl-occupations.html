AITSL occupations and ANZSCO codes for teachers

Seven school teacher occupations. Pick one to see who it fits.

ANZSCO 241213

Primary School Teacher

Fits a teaching qualification with initial teacher education for primary schooling and supervised practice in a primary setting.

AITSL does not recommend an occupation. Choose the one your teaching qualification fits best, then follow its own checklist.

ANZSCO 241311

Middle School Teacher

Fits a teaching qualification built around the middle years, with practice in a matching school setting.

AITSL does not recommend an occupation. Choose the one your teaching qualification fits best, then follow its own checklist.

ANZSCO 241411

Secondary School Teacher

Fits a secondary teaching qualification. The checklist asks for at least 45 days of supervised practice with students aged 13 to 18 in a secondary school.

AITSL does not recommend an occupation. Choose the one your teaching qualification fits best, then follow its own checklist.

ANZSCO 241511

Special Needs Teacher

Fits a specialist qualification for teaching students with learning difficulties or special needs, across early childhood to secondary.

AITSL does not recommend an occupation. Choose the one your teaching qualification fits best, then follow its own checklist.

ANZSCO 241512

Teacher of the Hearing Impaired

Fits a specialist qualification for teaching students who are deaf or hard of hearing.

AITSL does not recommend an occupation. Choose the one your teaching qualification fits best, then follow its own checklist.

ANZSCO 241513

Teacher of the Sight Impaired

Fits a specialist qualification for teaching students who are blind or have low vision.

AITSL does not recommend an occupation. Choose the one your teaching qualification fits best, then follow its own checklist.

ANZSCO 241599

Special Education Teachers nec

Fits a specialist qualification in a distinct special education field that is not covered by the Special Needs, hearing impaired or sight impaired occupations.

AITSL does not recommend an occupation. Choose the one your teaching qualification fits best, then follow its own checklist.

Early childhood teachers: AITSL stopped accepting Early Childhood (Pre-primary School) Teacher applications after 6 December 2024. ACECQA now assesses that occupation.

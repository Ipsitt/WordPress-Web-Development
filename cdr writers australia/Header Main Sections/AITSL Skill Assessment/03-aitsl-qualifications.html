AITSL qualification requirements: four years of study and initial teacher education

AITSL assesses the level, length and content of your higher education.

01

4 years of university study

At least four years full-time, or the part-time equivalent, and it must include a relevant initial teacher education qualification.

02

Teaching qualification at AQF Level 7

Comparable to an Australian Bachelor degree or higher, with at least one year full-time of teacher education for your nominated schooling stage.

03

Other study at AQF Level 6

Non-teaching higher education must be comparable to an Advanced Diploma or Associate Degree or higher, and all completed qualifications are listed.

04

Supervised teaching practice

Compulsory and formally assessed. The secondary checklist asks for at least 45 days with students aged 13 to 18, so check your own occupation's checklist.

What AITSL does not assess

Short courses under one year full-timeNon-academic qualificationsProfessional development programsIncomplete qualificationsTESOL, TEFL or ESL for migrationMontessori as the teacher education

AITSL does not consider work experience, whether it comes before or after your qualification. Teacher registration is not needed to apply, and a suitable outcome does not let you teach, because state and territory authorities handle registration.

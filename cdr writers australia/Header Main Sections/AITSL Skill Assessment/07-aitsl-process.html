How the AITSL skills assessment application works

Seven stages from checking your visa to receiving your outcome. Click a stage or use the buttons.

Step 1 of 7

Confirm your visa pathway

Ask the Department of Home Affairs whether your visa needs a skills assessment. AITSL cannot advise on visa questions and will not give a pre-assessment opinion.

Step 2 of 7

Pick your occupation

Select the teacher occupation your qualification fits, and complete that occupation's checklist and the Application Guide.

Step 3 of 7

Gather and scan documents

Collect identity documents, certificates, transcripts, the university supervised practice statement and English evidence.

Step 4 of 7

Apply in the Applicant Portal

Register, complete the form, upload documents and accept the terms. You cannot add documents after submission unless AITSL asks.

Step 5 of 7

Pay the fee

The fee is paid online in the portal after the form is complete, by Visa or MasterCard only.

Step 6 of 7

Track your status

There is no fast-track, no phone enquiries and no progress emails. The portal dashboard shows status and AITSL emails if it needs more.

Step 7 of 7

Receive your result

AITSL emails you when your application is finalised, and you view the outcome in My eQuals. A suitable result can lead to an optional Skilled Employment Statement.

AITSL emails from migration@aitsl.edu.au or no-reply@teachermigration.aitsl.edu.au, so add both to your safe senders.

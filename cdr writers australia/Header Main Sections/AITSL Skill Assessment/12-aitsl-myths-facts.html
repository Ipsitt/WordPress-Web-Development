AITSL skills assessment myths and facts

Eight things applicants often get wrong.

Myth

Work experience will strengthen my AITSL result.

Fact

AITSL's assessment is qualifications-based. Work experience is not considered, though it can support a separate Skilled Employment Statement.

Myth

I must send certified copies.

Fact

Certified true copies are not required, although AITSL may ask for originals. Send clear colour scans.

Myth

PTE is accepted like IELTS.

Fact

The July 2026 checklist names Academic IELTS only, with 7.0 reading and writing and 8.0 listening and speaking.

Myth

I need teacher registration first.

Fact

It is not needed to apply, and a suitable outcome does not let you teach.

Myth

Early childhood teachers apply to AITSL.

Fact

Since 7 December 2024, ACECQA assesses that occupation.

Myth

I can pay extra to speed it up.

Fact

There is no fast-track, no phone enquiry line and no progress updates.

Myth

TESOL or TEFL will count.

Fact

AITSL does not assess these for migration.

Myth

Results arrive by post in 10 to 12 weeks.

Fact

Results arrive by email and My eQuals, usually within 4 weeks, sometimes up to 6.

AITSL skills assessment FAQs: fees, processing time, English and supervised teaching

Straight answers to what teachers search before they apply.

How much does an AITSL skills assessment cost?
From 7 September 2026, the AITSL skills assessment application costs A$1,198, an appeal costs A$938 and a Skilled Employment Statement costs A$265. You pay online in the Applicant Portal by Visa or MasterCard, and AITSL states no GST is charged on the assessment fee.
How long does an AITSL skills assessment take?
AITSL says most assessment-ready applications finish within 4 weeks and some take up to 6. Incomplete applications, incorrect documents and peak periods cause delays, and there is no fast-track service.
Which teacher occupations does AITSL assess?
AITSL assesses Primary School Teacher 241213, Middle School Teacher 241311, Secondary School Teacher 241411, Special Needs Teacher 241511, Teacher of the Hearing Impaired 241512, Teacher of the Sight Impaired 241513 and Special Education Teachers nec 241599.
Does AITSL still assess early childhood teachers?
No. AITSL stopped accepting Early Childhood (Pre-primary School) Teacher applications after 6 December 2024, and ACECQA now assesses that occupation.
What are the AITSL English language requirements?
You meet either the study option or the test option. The test option is Academic IELTS with at least 7.0 in reading and writing and 8.0 in speaking and listening on a single report taken within the last 3 years. The study option is 4 full years of university study in Australia, Canada, Ireland, New Zealand, the UK or the USA.
How many years of study does AITSL require?
At least 4 years full-time, or the part-time equivalent, of university-level study that includes a relevant initial teacher education qualification. The teaching qualification must be comparable to an Australian Bachelor degree or higher.
How many supervised teaching days does AITSL require?
Supervised teaching practice is compulsory and formally assessed. The secondary school checklist asks for at least 45 days with students aged 13 to 18 in a secondary school, so read the checklist for your own occupation.
Does AITSL need certified copies of my documents?
No. Certified true copies are not required, although AITSL may ask for originals. Upload colour scans at 300 dpi or higher as PDF, PNG or JPEG, up to 20 MB each, and include accredited English translations beside originals that are not in English.
Does AITSL consider my teaching work experience?
No. The skills assessment is qualifications-based. If you want to claim employment points, you can apply for an optional Skilled Employment Statement after a suitable outcome.
How long is an AITSL skills assessment valid?
It is valid for the period shown on your outcome statement from the issue date and cannot be renewed. AITSL's Application Guide has described this period as two years, so check your own statement.
Can I appeal an unsuitable AITSL outcome?
Yes. Email AITSL to clarify the reasons first, then request an appeal within 60 days of the outcome date. The appeal fee is A$938, and new evidence gained after the outcome needs a new application.
Do I need teacher registration for an AITSL skills assessment?
No. Registration is not required to apply, and a suitable outcome does not allow you to teach. State and territory authorities handle teacher registration.

AITSL Application Format & Requirements

The AITSL assessment process generally involves reviewing:

Eligibility Checklist

  • Correct ANZSCO occupation selection
  • Recognized teaching qualification
  • Required supervised teaching practice
  • English language requirements (if applicable)
  • Proper documentation for skills assessment

Key Assessment Areas

  • Academic qualification level comparable to Australian standards
  • Supervised teaching practice (age range & duration)
  • Curriculum focus and teaching methodology
  • English language requirements (if required)
  • Proper documentation format and certified copies

What is the APS skills assessment for psychologists?

The Australian Psychological Society (APS) is the only national assessing authority for overseas-trained psychologists applying for skilled migration. An APS skills assessment compares your psychology study with an Australian six-year sequence and results in a Statement of Psychology Qualifications that goes with your visa application.

You may also see it called an APS skill assessment, an APS psychology skills assessment or a psychology skills assessment for Australia. It is separate from registering with the Psychology Board of Australia and AHPRA, which is what lets you practise.

A properly prepared psychology skills assessment application improves clarity and helps reduce delays during the assessment process. We provide structured guidance to help applicants prepare strong and well-organized APS skill assessment applications.

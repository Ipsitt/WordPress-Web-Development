Which APS skills assessment pathway fits your psychology qualifications?

APS compares your study with an Australian six-year sequence. Pick the route that matches your training.

Comparable to six years of Australian study

Your qualifications must be comparable to a six-year sequence of psychology study in Australia. APS looks at content, structure and the competencies you can demonstrate, not just the number of years.

Levels 1 and 2 plus a postgraduate psychology degree

You are expected to show Level 1 and Level 2 competencies, then a postgraduate psychology degree. APS may assess it at Level 3 if it includes advanced coursework, research and supervised professional practice.

Advanced courseworkResearchSupervised practice

A three-year sequence followed by a PhD

The other combination APS accepts is a minimum three-year psychology sequence at Level 1 followed by a PhD in psychology. Research-only study does not replace the supervised practice that professional-level training requires.

Unconditional general registration

If you already hold unconditional general registration with AHPRA, that is an alternative to the six-year comparison. Overseas-qualified psychologists first apply for provisional registration, and general registration follows a Transitional Program set by the Psychology Board of Australia.

APS English language requirements for the psychology skills assessment

English proof is part of every APS skilled migration application unless you qualify for an exemption.

01

IELTS Academic

Overall 7, with 7 in listening, reading and speaking and 6.5 in writing.

02

PTE Academic

Overall 63, with 58 in listening, 59 in reading, 60 in writing and 76 in speaking.

03

Two-year window

Test results must be obtained within two years before you lodge your application.

04

Exemption

Applicants who completed all psychology training in English at a tertiary institution in Australia, New Zealand, Ireland, the UK, Canada or the USA.

Accepted
  • IELTS Academic or PTE Academic score reports as colour scans.
  • Two sittings within six months, if each meets the overall minimum and the combined scores meet every component.
  • IELTS One Skill Retake for APS skilled migration applications.
Not accepted
  • At-home or online tests such as IELTS Online, PTE Academic Online and TOEFL iBT Home Edition.
  • OET@Home and the IELTS Indicator.
  • Component scores below 6.5 in IELTS, or below 53, 54, 60 and 66 in PTE for two-sitting results.

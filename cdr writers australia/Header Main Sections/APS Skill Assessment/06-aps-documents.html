APS skills assessment documents checklist

APS only assesses completed qualifications, and it does not hold an application open while you gather documents.

Your readiness

What APS will not accept

Avoid these common slips, which can stall or sink an application.

  • Certified copies. APS asks for colour scans of your originals.
  • University letters as proof of completion.
  • Unofficial transcripts or transcripts from incomplete qualifications.
  • Draft theses. APS needs the final version.
  • Affidavits as evidence of a name change.

Recent graduates without a degree certificate can submit a diploma supplement or provisional certificate, which APS assesses case by case. A PhD under examination cannot be assessed until it is conferred.

APS skilled employment statement: when your work experience counts for points

If you want to claim employment points, APS can assess your work history separately from your qualifications.

≠
Skills assessment
  • Assesses your completed psychology qualifications against the six-year sequence.
  • Does not assess work experience.
  • Gives you the Statement of Psychology Qualifications for your visa.
Skilled employment statement
  • Optional, and only available after a positive skills assessment.
  • States whether your work equals the skilled level of an Australian psychologist.
  • Supports employment points that the Department of Home Affairs awards.

You meet one of these within the last 10 years

01

1 year in Australia

At least one year as a general registered psychologist in Australia within the last 10 years.

02

3 years registered abroad

At least three years as a registered psychologist in a country with state or national registration, within the last 10 years.

03

3 years in a psychological role

At least three years in a psychological role since your highest qualification, in a country without psychologist registration.

04

Research degree route

A research-only postgraduate psychology degree plus at least one year in an academic role in Australia, or three years overseas.

You must also be under the age limit for your visa subclass and show at least 20 hours a week of relevant employment. Acceptable evidence includes position descriptions, contracts, tax returns and payment summaries.

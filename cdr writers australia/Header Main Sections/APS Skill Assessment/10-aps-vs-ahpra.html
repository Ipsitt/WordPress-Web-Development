APS skills assessment vs AHPRA registration for overseas psychologists

A positive APS outcome opens the visa door. It does not by itself open the registration door.

≠
APS migration assessment
  • Required by the Department of Home Affairs for a skilled visa in a listed psychology occupation.
  • Produces a Statement of Psychology Qualifications with your ANZSCO code if found suitable.
  • Does not itself let you practise as a psychologist.
AHPRA registration
  • Applied for directly with the Psychology Board of Australia, not through APS.
  • Overseas-qualified psychologists start with provisional registration.
  • General registration follows a Transitional Program, and a positive APS result does not guarantee it.

APS skills assessment FAQs: fees, processing time, English and occupations

Straight answers to what psychologists search before they apply.

What is the APS skills assessment?
It is the skilled migration assessment that the Australian Psychological Society (APS) runs for overseas-trained psychologists. APS compares your psychology qualifications with an Australian six-year sequence and issues a Statement of Psychology Qualifications that goes with your visa application.
Which occupations does APS assess?
APS assesses Clinical Psychologist (272311), Educational Psychologist (272312), Organisational Psychologist (272313) and Psychologist nec (272399). The first three need the matching AHPRA area-of-practice endorsement, and without one the result is under 272399.
How much does an APS skills assessment cost?
From 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027, the skilled migration assessment costs AUD 1,388 (GST-free) if you apply from outside Australia and AUD 1,527 including GST within Australia. The skilled employment statement costs AUD 927 or AUD 1,020. Fees are non-refundable and may change after 30 June 2027.
How long does an APS skills assessment take?
APS gives an estimate of about 12 weeks, which may increase with demand. Applications are processed in order of receipt and APS will not expedite them for deadlines.
How long is an APS skills assessment valid?
APS states that its statements are valid for 10 years from the issue date, and it does not reissue outcomes that are 10 or more years old. Your English test results must still be within two years when you lodge.
What are the APS English language requirements?
IELTS Academic needs an overall 7 with 7 in listening, reading and speaking and 6.5 in writing. PTE Academic needs an overall 63 with 58 in listening, 59 in reading, 60 in writing and 76 in speaking. Online and at-home tests are not accepted.
Does APS assess work experience?
No. The standard skills assessment only assesses completed qualifications. If you want employment points, you can apply for a separate skilled employment statement after you receive a positive skills assessment.
Does six years of study guarantee a positive APS result?
No. APS looks at content, structure and demonstrated competencies, so six years of overseas study does not automatically count as comparable to an Australian sequence.
Does APS accept certified copies of my documents?
APS asks for high-quality colour scans of your original documents and states that certified copies are not accepted. It also does not accept university letters, unofficial transcripts, draft theses or affidavits for name changes.
Is an APS skills assessment the same as AHPRA registration?
No. The APS assessment supports a skilled visa application. Registration to practise is handled by the Psychology Board of Australia and AHPRA, and overseas-qualified psychologists start with provisional registration.
Can I apply for an APS assessment from overseas?
Yes. You can apply online from outside Australia without waiting until you move, but you must lodge a complete application because APS does not hold incomplete ones.
Can I ask APS to reassess my outcome?
Yes, if you have new official evidence, new qualifications from the last 10 years, AHPRA general registration gained since your assessment, or a request from the Department of Home Affairs. Reassessment costs AUD 300 and takes about 12 weeks.

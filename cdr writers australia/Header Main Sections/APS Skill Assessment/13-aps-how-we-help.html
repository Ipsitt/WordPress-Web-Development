How we support your APS skills assessment

Practical guidance at each stage. You stay in control of your own application.

Pathway and code check

We help you work out whether the six-year comparison or the registration route fits, and which ANZSCO code your endorsement supports.

Qualification mapping

We help you line up your subjects and placements with the APAC levels APS looks for.

Document set review

We check your scans, transcripts and thesis details against APS's documentation requirements.

English evidence check

We confirm your test type, scores and dates fall inside APS's rules before you lodge.

Pre-lodgement review

We look for gaps and inconsistencies, because APS cannot hold an incomplete application.

Reassessment guidance

If you need a reassessment, we help you structure the cover letter and new evidence.

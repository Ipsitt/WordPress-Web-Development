What is CPD?

Continuing Professional Development (CPD) is the process that will keep you on trend with the recent happenings of engineering news after your undergraduate qualification. It is used for documenting your skills, knowledge, and experience gained in your training years.

CPD Report list should include:

  • Information on formal postgraduate study
  • Conferences you've attended (short courses, workshops, or seminars).
  • Content you've created and presented for symposia and gatherings.
  • Any volunteer or mentorship work related to your profession.
  • Books, magazines, and manuals you've reviewed.

What are Career Episodes?

Career Episodes explain your educational qualification and work experience in the field of engineering. All three are based on specific engineering projects done over a certain period of time.

They give detailed information on technical skills applied to the project described. Each should be 1,000 to 2,500 words long.

  • A project you worked on
  • A specific position you performed in your firm
  • A specific engineering challenge you addressed

Format structure:

  • Introduction (100 words)
  • Background (200-500 words)
  • Engineering Activity (500-1000 words)
  • Summary (50-100 words)

What is a CDR Report?

Competency Demonstration Reports is a technical report which is submitted by students to Engineers Australia for their assessment and verification.

Engineers who are planning to migrate to Australia for education or career should write CDR and submit it to Engineers Australia for their skill and knowledge assessment. Engineers Australia is the body of professional and knowledgeable Engineers. They approve CDR Reports only when they are written as per the guidelines provided by EA in the MSA Booklet. Also, your reports should grammatically correct and should not contain any plagiarism to be successfully approved by EA.

The assessment by CDR Australia is largely focused on the applicant's undergraduate qualification and graduate abilities. For Australian immigration, CDR EA recognizes four categories:

Professional Engineers

Expected to manage engineering projects that benefit society and business. Must comprehend project expenses, dangers, and limits, and ensure reliable operation of technology. Four years of a Bachelor's degree in Engineering is required.

Engineering Technologist

Experts in a certain discipline of engineering technology. Must be up to date on current technologies but are not required to have the same breadth of vision as Professional Engineers. Three-year Bachelor's degree/diploma required.

Engineering Associate

Commonly referred to as 'paraprofessionals'. Intimately aware of standards, codes of practice, and safely operating plants/equipment. Responsibilities range from feasibility to procurement. Two-year diploma or degree required.

Engineering Manager

Develop goals, policies, and plans, as well as supervise engineering activities. A senior executive post. Requires seven years of experience as a Professional Engineer and at least three years at the Engineering Manager level.

Why is it required?

CDR is submitted by the graduate engineers who are willing to migrate to Australia for post-graduation courses or permanent residency purposes to Engineers Australia.

All engineering colleges will expect their students to be knowledgeable, intellectual and versatile. CDR will give an insight into the mindset, knowledge and understanding capacities of the international students. Professional colleges which impart post-graduation courses in various engineering fields will be able to understand the skill-sets of the international students. Without CDR it will be tough for the EA to assess the knowledge and skills of the students.

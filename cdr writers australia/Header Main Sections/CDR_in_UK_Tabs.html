Three Career Episode Report

In CDR Writing Services there are three Career Episode they are categorized as:

  • Career Episode Based on Work Experience
  • Career Episode Based on Academic Project
  • Career Episode Based on Training Project

It is not a joke to write these three-episode reports, they are the main and most important part of the CDR Report. Each episode contains 1000 to 2500 words. Each should explain detailed engineering skills, video projects, working experience, problem-solving skills and other information about the applicant. Career Episode should focus on the applicant work and responsibility, not the teamwork. These skills should relate to the job responsibility of the engineers in the respective fields when they work in Australia. EA should approve of them by showing these three career episode reports.

CPA Australia skills assessment for migration: what accountants need to know

CPA Australia is an authorised assessing authority for accountants applying for skilled migration to Australia. A CPA skills assessment first checks that your degree is equivalent to an Australian bachelor's degree. It then checks that your studies cover the mandatory accounting knowledge areas for your nominated occupation, and that your English meets the standard unless you are exempt.

Once your qualification receives a suitable outcome, you can ask for a separate skilled employment assessment of your accounting work experience. Getting each stage right the first time keeps your visa timeline on track.

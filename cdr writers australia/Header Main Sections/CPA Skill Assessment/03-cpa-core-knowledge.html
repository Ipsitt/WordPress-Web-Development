CPA Australia qualification assessment and core knowledge areas

CPA looks at the level of your degree, then at what it actually taught you.

Bachelor-level equivalence

Your qualification must be assessed as equivalent to at least an Australian bachelor's degree, using guidance from the federal employment and education departments.

Relevance to your occupation

CPA then judges how relevant your studies are to the occupation you nominated. If your course appears on CPA's accredited course list, you do not need to supply a syllabus.

The seven core knowledge areas

Accounting systems and processes
Business law
Economics
Financial accounting and reporting
Finance and financial management
Management accounting
Quantitative methods

Taxation Accountant

Your studies must also cover Australian taxation law, on top of the seven core areas.

External Auditor

Your studies must also cover audit and assurance, on top of the seven core areas.

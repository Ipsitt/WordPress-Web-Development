Tests taken before 7 August 2025 are judged against older thresholds, for example 65 in each PTE Academic skill. Check CPA's English page for your test date.

Exempt passports

Holders of a valid passport from Canada, New Zealand, the Republic of Ireland, the United Kingdom or the USA are exempt, for applications submitted after 7 May 2026.

Validity

A test result is valid for three years from the test date, and it must still be in date when your outcome is issued. The Accounting Professional Year Program has no expiry for assessment purposes.

Not accepted

At-home or online tests, and TOEFL iBT results from 26 July 2023 to 5 May 2024. Your PTE report must show both the Score Report Code and Registration ID.

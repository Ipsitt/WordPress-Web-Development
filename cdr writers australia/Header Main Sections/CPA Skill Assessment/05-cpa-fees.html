Who you pay

The assessment fee is paid to CPA Australia, the assessing authority, through its online application portal using an account in the applicant's own name. A migration agent can manage the application from their own CPA account, but the assessment fee still goes to CPA. Any fee from a consultancy for preparing your documents is separate.

Withdrawals and refunds

If you withdraw before any work begins, an administration fee applies ($85 onshore, $77 offshore, $84 Singapore). Once preliminary work has started, the assessment fee is not refunded.

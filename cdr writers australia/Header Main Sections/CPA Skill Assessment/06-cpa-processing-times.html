CPA Australia processing time: standard and Fast Track assessment

Times are indicative and can change with application volumes.

~10 daysstandard processing, in business days
~5 daysFast Track, in business days
6 monthsto answer a CPA request before the application is deactivated

The clock starts only when CPA holds your complete set of documents. Weekends, public holidays and the end-of-year office closure do not count. If CPA asks for more information, your application goes on hold and the clock restarts when the information arrives. Fast Track applies to qualification assessments only, including additional ANZSCO qualification assessments, and not to skilled employment.

Documents required for a CPA Australia skills assessment

What you need depends on your qualifications, but most applicants provide the items below.

Key documents

  • IdentityPassport photo page or national ID card, plus official evidence of any name change.
  • Degree and transcriptsOfficial certificates and transcripts, including earlier studies for which you received exemptions. My eQuals users need the signed PDF showing the blue ribbon.
  • Professional body resultsMembership certificate and marksheets for every level you completed or were exempted from, where relevant.
  • SyllabusAn official syllabus for each non-accredited qualification, as one PDF. A witnessed statutory declaration is accepted only in exceptional cases.
  • English evidenceA recognised test report or the Accounting Professional Year Program certificate and transcript, unless you are exempt.
  • Chinese degreesVerification from an official Chinese verification service, sent directly to CPA, with an official English translation.
  • Third-party formA signed authorisation form if an agent or other third party manages your application.

Document standards

  • Scans of originalsClear, high-resolution colour scans. Certified copies are not accepted.
  • PrivacyBlack out credit card numbers, bank account numbers and tax file numbers.
  • TranslationsNon-English awards and transcripts need an official translation, supplied with the original.
  • File namingOne file per document, named with your full name and the document title, for example "Last Name, First Name - Passport".
  • Upload togetherSubmit every document at once to avoid delays.
Applications suspected of fraud or misleading information can be refused without a refund and reported to the Department of Home Affairs.

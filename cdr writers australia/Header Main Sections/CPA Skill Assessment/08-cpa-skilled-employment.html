CPA skilled employment assessment requirements

CPA does assess work experience, but only after your qualification receives a suitable outcome. You can apply for both together as a combined assessment.

10 yearsemployment window before your application date
20 hrs / weekminimum, paid and continuous
1 yeartotal accumulated employment needed
3 monthsminimum for each role you claim
After degreeonly work after your bachelor-equivalent qualification counts
Paid onlyvolunteering and study placements, including the Professional Year, do not count

What the employer testimonial must show

  • Official letterhead with full address, phone, email and website
  • Name, position and signature of an authorised signatory, not a colleague at the same level or below
  • The author's direct work email and phone number, not personal contacts
  • Start and end dates for each role in DD/MM/YYYY format
  • Duties in the employer's own words, specific to daily and monthly tasks
  • Permanent or temporary status, weekly hours and annual salary

Pay, tax and self-employment evidence

  • PayslipsAt least three per claimed role, ideally from the start, middle and end.
  • Self-employed tax returnsScans for the first, middle and final year of the business.
  • Self-employed extrasBusiness registration, practising certificate, a statutory declaration and references from two or more clients on letterhead.
CVs are not accepted as employment evidence, and keeping your own business's books may not count as accounting experience.

Official source: CPA Australia skilled employment assessment. Details checked 12 October 2026, so confirm current figures with CPA Australia before you apply.

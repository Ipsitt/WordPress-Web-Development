Mistakes that delay or sink a CPA Australia skills assessment

Most problems come from the paperwork, not from the applicant's skills. Check these six before you submit.

Poor-quality scans

Cropped, blurred or pixelated scans are a common reason documents are rejected.

Certified copies

CPA wants high-resolution colour scans of originals, not certified copies.

My eQuals without the ribbon

Electronic transcripts and awards must be the signed PDFs that show the blue ribbon.

At-home English tests

Only tests taken at authorised test centres are accepted, and results must be verifiable.

Generic job duties

Duties copied from ANZSCO or written in generic terms are rejected. Use the employer's own words.

The wrong signatory

A testimonial signed by a colleague at the same level or below, or a CV in its place, does not meet the standard.

After your CPA assessment outcome: validity, further study, update and review

Your outcome letter is the start of the next stage, not the end of the process.

Know your validity

Your application is valid for three years from the date of your first outcome. Updates, extra ANZSCO codes, employment assessments and reviews do not extend it. After expiry, you start a new application.

Close any study gap

If the outcome says further study is needed, complete the missing areas through CPA Program foundation exams or accredited courses from other providers, then apply for a qualification update.

Update or add codes

Within the validity period you can update your file with new study, a new English test or extra work experience, or add further ANZSCO codes.

Request a review

If you believe the outcome is wrong, ask for a review within 12 months of the outcome date. A review uses only your original documents. New documents make it an update.

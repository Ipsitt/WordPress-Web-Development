CPA Australia skills assessment FAQs

Quick answers to the questions accountants ask most.

What does CPA Australia assess for migration?
CPA Australia assesses whether your accounting qualification is equivalent to an Australian bachelor's degree and covers the core knowledge areas for your nominated occupation. It also checks English proficiency unless you are exempt, and can assess your skilled employment separately.
Which occupations does CPA Australia assess?
Accountant (General) 221111, Management Accountant 221112, Taxation Accountant 221113, External Auditor 221213, Corporate Treasurer 221212 and Finance Manager 132211.
Does CPA Australia assess work experience?
Yes. It offers a separate skilled employment assessment, but only once your qualification has received a suitable outcome. You can apply for both together with the combined assessment option.
How long does a CPA Australia skills assessment take?
Standard processing is about 10 business days and Fast Track qualification assessments take about 5 business days. The clock starts once CPA has your complete documents, and times are indicative.
How much does a CPA Australia skills assessment cost?
A standard qualification assessment costs $565 onshore or $514 offshore, and Fast Track costs $675 onshore or $614 offshore. Additional ANZSCO codes, updates, skilled employment and reviews are priced separately.
Do I need an English test for a CPA Australia assessment?
Yes, unless you hold a valid passport from Canada, New Zealand, the Republic of Ireland, the United Kingdom or the USA, or you completed the Accounting Professional Year Program. Tests must be taken at an authorised test centre.
How long is a CPA Australia skills assessment valid?
Three years from the date of your first outcome. Later updates, extra occupations, employment assessments and reviews do not reset that date.
What if my outcome says I need further study?
Complete the missing areas through CPA Program foundation exams or accredited courses elsewhere, then apply for a paid qualification update within the validity period.
Can I apply for a provisional skills assessment?
No. CPA Australia stopped offering provisional skills assessments from 15 June 2024, following changes to the Temporary Graduate visa programs.
Can a migration agent lodge my CPA application?
Yes. Third parties and migration agents can manage an application from their own CPA account, with a signed third-party authorisation form from you. You should still create your own account with your own details.
Can I ask for a review of my CPA outcome?
Yes. Lodge a review within 12 months of the outcome date. It considers only the documents you originally submitted, and the review fee is refunded if the original outcome was wrong.

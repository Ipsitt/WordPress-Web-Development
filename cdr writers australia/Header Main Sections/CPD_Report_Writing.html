Elements of CPD described by Engineers Australia for Evaluation

The Engineers Australia Society has described four elements of continuing professional development. Your report will be evaluated based off of these elements.

Get Free Samples

Personal Commitment

This describes your ethical behaviour in your workplace and how you handle responsibilities for the engineering activities you’ve performed.

Value of Workplace

This defines your roles on the way you communicate at the workplace and reflect your decision-making abilities.

Obligations to the Community

Your report will be assessed based on how well you carry the community activities or willing to undergo risk and develop sustainable solutions that will last for a long time.

Technical Proficiency

Engineering skills, creativity, knowledge and innovation etc. are some of the basic elements you have to reflect in your CPD.

What is Engineers Australia Skill Assessment?

Engineers Australia (EA) is the designated assessing authority for engineering occupations under Australia’s skilled migration program. An Engineers Australia skill assessment evaluates whether your qualifications and engineering competencies meet Australian standards for your nominated occupation.

For applicants with non-accredited qualifications, the assessment generally requires submission of a Competency Demonstration Report (CDR). We provide structured guidance to help you prepare a well-organized, properly aligned, and clearly documented application. Assessment decisions are made solely by Engineers Australia.

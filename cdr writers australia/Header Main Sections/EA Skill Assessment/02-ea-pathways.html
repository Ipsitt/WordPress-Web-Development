Engineers Australia Assessment Pathways

Your qualification decides the pathway. Hover or tap each one to see who it fits and what it costs.

Australian qualification

Your program must be EA-accredited, and you must have started it during or after the accreditation commencement year. This is the lowest-fee pathway at A$346.50 including GST.

Accord recognition depends on the specific program and its accreditation period, not just the country. Check the IEA qualification checker before you choose.

Washington Accord

For qualifications from accredited programs in signatory countries, completed in or after the year the country became a full signatory. French qualifications are assessed under this Accord. Fee A$555.50 including GST.

Accord recognition depends on the specific program and its accreditation period, not just the country. Check the IEA qualification checker before you choose.

Sydney Accord

For engineering technologist qualifications from accredited programs that fall within the signatory's listed accreditation period. Provisional accreditation is not enough. Fee A$555.50 including GST.

Accord recognition depends on the specific program and its accreditation period, not just the country. Check the IEA qualification checker before you choose.

Dublin Accord

For engineering associate qualifications from fully accredited programs. From 1 September 2024, advanced diploma and associate degree holders qualify on the accredited route only when the program is EA-accredited. Fee A$555.50 including GST.

Accord recognition depends on the specific program and its accreditation period, not just the country. Check the IEA qualification checker before you choose.

CDR pathway

For provisionally accredited Australian programs, non-accredited qualifications, an occupation different from your accredited title, or qualifications showing underpinning knowledge. You prove competence through career episodes. Standard fee A$1,034 including GST.

Accord recognition depends on the specific program and its accreditation period, not just the country. Check the IEA qualification checker before you choose.

Plan before you start: EA warns that the right pathway should be settled first, because documents, fees and evidence differ for each one.

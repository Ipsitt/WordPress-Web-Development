How to apply for an Engineers Australia skill assessment, step by step

EA describes six stages. Click through them or use the buttons.

Step 1 of 6

Check your qualification

Country of study, qualification type and accreditation status set your pathway. Use EA's accredited programs list or the IEA qualification checker for Accord programs.

Step 2 of 6

Understand the four categories

Professional engineer, engineering technologist, engineering associate or engineering manager. Your qualification and skills decide where you sit.

Step 3 of 6

Choose an assessment pathway

Australian, Washington, Sydney, Dublin or CDR. Settle this before you start, because the evidence differs.

Step 4 of 6

Prepare your application

Read the current Prepare your Migration Skills Assessment application guide, gather documents and, for CDR, write your episodes.

Step 5 of 6

Create an account and submit

Create a portal account and receive your EA ID by email. Choose migration skills assessment, select your pathway, upload documents, pay the fee and submit. You can save and return.

Step 6 of 6

Receive your outcome

EA emails the result. A successful applicant gets an outcome letter you can verify through the portal, and EA does not give migration advice or award points.

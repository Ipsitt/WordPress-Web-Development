Engineers Australia Processing Time

The 15 weeks and 20 business days measure time to assessor assignment, not the date you get your letter.

15 weeks

Wait for assessor

Standard applications are generally assigned to an assessor within about 15 weeks.

20 days

Fast-track assignment

With fast track, assignment to an assessor within 20 business days. It does not promise an outcome by then.

30 days

Gap window

If EA finds missing items, you generally get one 30-day chance to fix them.

3 yrs

English test age

Your test must be taken within three years before lodging and stay verifiable.

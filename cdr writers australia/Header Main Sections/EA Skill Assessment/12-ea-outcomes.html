Engineers Australia outcome letter, reviews and appeals

What you receive, what it is not, and what you can do if you disagree.

Successful outcome
  • EA emails you the result.
  • You receive an outcome letter showing your category and occupation.
  • You can verify the letter through the EA portal.
  • The letter has no expiry with EA, but Home Affairs decides how long it is accepted for a visa.
If you disagree
  • Request a review, listed at A$368.50 including GST.
  • Escalate to an appeal, listed at A$704 including GST.
  • An updated outcome letter costs A$181.50 including GST.
  • You need an updated letter for outcomes older than three years, or to change title, name or address.

EA does not give migration advice and does not award migration points. The Department of Home Affairs handles visa eligibility and points.

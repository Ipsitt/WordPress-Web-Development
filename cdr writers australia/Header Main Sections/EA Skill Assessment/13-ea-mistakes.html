Common Engineers Australia application mistakes that cause delays

Six issues EA's own guidance warns about.

01

Choosing the wrong pathway

Documents and fees differ for each route, so settle the pathway before you start.

02

Relying on a lapsed or old English test

The test must be within three years of lodging and still verifiable.

03

Using the wrong score table

Scores for tests taken before and after 7 August 2025 are different.

04

Weak employment evidence

Letters without duties, dates or a signed endorsement leave gaps, and bank statements or payslips do not count as secondary proof.

05

Having someone else write your episodes

EA prohibits third parties writing career episodes or the summary statement, with penalties including rejection and a 12, 24 or 36-month ban.

06

Skipping paragraph numbers

Number every paragraph so the summary statement can point to exact evidence.

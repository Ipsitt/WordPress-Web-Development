Engineers Australia claims you may see online, checked

We compared common statements with Engineers Australia's own pages and its January 2026 guide.

ClaimWhat we found
Standard CDR fee A$1,034 including GSTConfirmed by Engineers Australia (A$940 excluding GST)
Washington, Sydney or Dublin fee A$555.50, Australian A$346.50, fast track A$396Confirmed by Engineers Australia
Standard assessment takes 8 to 12 weeksNot an EA figure. EA says assignment to an assessor generally takes 15 weeks
Fast track returns a result in about 20 working daysNot accurate. EA says assignment to an assessor within 20 business days, with no outcome guarantee
Accredited qualifications use the MSA pathwayMisleading. MSA means the whole migration skills assessment, and accredited routes are Australian, Washington, Sydney or Dublin
Career episodes run 1,000 to 2,500 wordsConfirmed by EA's career episodes guide
An MSA Booklet 2026 existsNot found. The latest booklet-titled edition we saw is dated March 2020, and the current guide is the January 2026 application guide
A positive assessment guarantees a visa or awards pointsFalse. EA gives neither migration advice nor points

Checked against EA's migration skills assessment page, fees page, application guide v1.1 (January 2026) and career episodes guide (June 2025).

Engineers Australia skill assessment FAQs: fees, documents and timing

Straight answers to what engineers search before they apply.

How much is the EA assessment fee?
For 2026 to 2027, Engineers Australia lists A$346.50 for an Australian accredited qualification, A$555.50 for Washington, Sydney or Dublin Accord qualifications, and A$1,034 for a standard CDR, all including GST.
What is the skill assessment Australia fee for engineers who need employment assessed?
Adding a relevant skilled employment assessment brings a CDR to A$1,512.50 including GST. Adding an overseas PhD assessment instead brings it to A$1,336.50, and adding both brings it to A$1,815.
How much does fast track cost?
Fast track adds A$396 including GST and is non-refundable. It gets your application to an assessor within 20 business days but does not guarantee an outcome in that time.
Is there an Engineers Australia skill assessment document checklist?
Yes. Accredited pathways need identity, photo, CV, English evidence, qualifications and transcripts. The CDR pathway adds a CPD statement, three career episodes, a summary statement and any registration certificate.
Where can I find the Engineers Australia skill assessment PDF?
EA publishes its application guide and online applicant guide as PDFs on its website. The current application guide is dated January 2026.
What is the MSA Booklet Engineers Australia used to publish?
It was a guide to the migration skills assessment, with editions in 2016, 2018, 2019 and 2020. EA now uses a guide titled Prepare your Migration Skills Assessment application.
Is there an Engineers Australia MSA Booklet 2026?
We found no 2026 document with that title. The January 2026 application guide replaces it for current rules.
How long does the assessment take?
EA says standard applications are generally assigned to an assessor within 15 weeks. That is not the time to an outcome, which depends on document quality and any requests for more information.
Do I need an English test?
Usually yes, unless you hold an exemption such as a UK, USA, Canada, New Zealand or Ireland passport and citizenship, or qualifying Australian study. The test must be taken within three years before lodging.
How many career episodes do I need?
Three, covering all 16 competency elements at least once across them, with a summary statement mapped to numbered paragraphs.
Can someone write my career episodes?
No. EA prohibits having a third party write your career episodes or summary statement. Guidance and review are different from authorship, and you must write in your own words.
What is on the skill assessment Australia list for engineers?
Engineers Australia assesses engineering managers, professionals, technologists and associates. On the CDR pathway you choose from 31 engineering occupations, and visa availability for each is set by Home Affairs.

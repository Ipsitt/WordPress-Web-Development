EA Application Format & Requirements

The Engineers Australia assessment process generally involves reviewing:

Eligibility Pathway

  • Accredited qualification pathway (Washington, Sydney, or Dublin Accord)
  • Non-accredited qualification pathway (CDR required)
We provide guidance to ensure your documentation aligns with Engineers Australia competency standards.

Competency Demonstration (if applicable)

  • Three Career Episodes
  • Summary Statement
  • Continuing Professional Development (CPD) list
We provide guidance to ensure your documentation aligns with Engineers Australia competency standards.

Employment & Qualification Review

  • Relevant engineering degree
  • Post-qualification employment experience (if applicable)
  • Duties aligned with ANZSCO occupation description
We provide guidance to ensure your documentation aligns with Engineers Australia competency standards.

Key Documents Typically Required

  • Academic degree certificate
  • Academic transcripts
  • English language test results (if required)
  • Career Episodes (for CDR pathway)
  • Summary Statement
  • CPD list
  • Employment reference letters
  • Resume/CV
  • Identity documents
We provide guidance to ensure your documentation aligns with Engineers Australia competency standards.

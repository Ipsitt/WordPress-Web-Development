What is an Employment Reference Letter?

It is the employer-issued proof of your work history, sometimes called an employment verification letter or work experience letter.

Purpose
Speaks to your character, work ethic and soft skills for a job or course application.
Confirms to an assessing authority what work you did, for how long and on what terms.
Written by
A manager, colleague or mentor, in their own personal style.
An authorised person at the employer, covering every field the authority asks for.
What it covers
Personality, strengths and a general recommendation.
Job title, exact dates, weekly hours, duties, pay and employment status.
Checked against
Nothing formal. It simply supports an application.
The ANZSCO occupation description, payslips, tax records and your CV.
If it is weak
A lukewarm impression.
A request for information (RFI) or a negative skills assessment outcome.

Employment verification is a technical document, not a testimonial. Assessors read it line by line to decide whether your experience really matches the occupation you nominated, so every detail has to be exact, consistent and checkable.

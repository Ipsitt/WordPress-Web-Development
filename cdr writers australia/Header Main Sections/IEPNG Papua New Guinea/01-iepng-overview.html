IEPNG competency based assessment for engineers in Papua New Guinea

The Institution of Engineers Papua New Guinea (IEPNG) is the professional body for engineers in PNG. Its Competency Based Assessment, usually shortened to CBA, is how engineers are assessed for IEPNG membership and for registration with the Professional Engineers Registration Board.

People look for it as IEPNG CBA, IEPNG CBA report help, IEPNG CBA report sample, IEPNG CBA report example or IEPNG registration. All of them lead to the same two-stage process, built on IEPNG's own forms and work samples, not an Engineers Australia CDR.

A clear, well-organised application helps assessors see your competence quickly. We help you plan your evidence and present your own work, so your submission reflects what you actually did.

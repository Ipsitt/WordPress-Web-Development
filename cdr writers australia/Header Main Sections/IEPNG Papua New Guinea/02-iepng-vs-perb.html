IEPNG membership vs PERB registration in Papua New Guinea

To practise engineering in PNG you need both. They are linked, but they are not the same thing.

≠
IEPNG membership
  • IEPNG is the professional body that represents engineers across all disciplines.
  • Members move through classes such as Graduate, Member and Fellow.
  • Its CBA process assesses your competence for membership.
PERB registration
  • The Professional Engineers Registration Board is a statutory board under the Professional Engineers Registration Act.
  • It registers and de-registers professional engineers.
  • Registration is a legal requirement to practise engineering in PNG.
How they connect: the PERB draws its members from the IEPNG Council, and the IEPNG President chairs it ex officio. A successful CBA leads to both your Membership and Registration certificates.

IEPNG membership classes and the usual career ladder

Most engineers move up through four classes. Other classes serve different roles.

1

Student

Entry class for engineering students, including interns.

2

Graduate

For graduates building experience. A structured Graduate Professional Development Program runs for at least four years, recorded in a log book.

3

Member

Full professional membership. This is the class that registration as a professional engineer is built around.

4

Fellow

For members with sustained professional achievement.

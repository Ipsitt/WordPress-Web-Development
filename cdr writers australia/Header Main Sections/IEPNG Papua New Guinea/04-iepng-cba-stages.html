The IEPNG CBA process: provisional membership, then full assessment

IEPNG runs the CBA in two stages. Hover a stage to see its steps.

1

Stage 1: Provisional Membership

Gets you onto the register while the full assessment is done

  • Complete Form CA01 or CA02, plus CA04 and CA05
  • Member Services checks documents and assigns a case officer
  • You pay the invoiced fees and send the receipt
  • Provisional Membership is issued, usually within about two weeks
2

Stage 2: Full CBA assessment

Where your competence is assessed in depth

  • Complete Form CA03 and submit two work samples
  • Two nominated referees each complete Form CA06
  • Assessor 1 reviews element by element, matched to your discipline
  • Assessor 2 reviews holistically, then the Coordinator and Council endorse

Fees are paid upfront, and IEPNG's Accounts team issues the invoices.

Inside IEPNG's full CBA review: from forms to Registered Engineer

Follow your Stage 2 application through IEPNG's review. Click a stage or use the buttons.

Step 1 of 7

Submit CA03 and work samples

You complete the Competence Self-Review (CA03) and submit two work samples.

Step 2 of 7

Two referees complete CA06

Two nominated referees each complete Form CA06 about your work.

Step 3 of 7

Member Services checks

Documents are checked against IEPNG's checklist and receipt of forms is registered.

Step 4 of 7

Element-by-element review

The first assessor, chosen for your engineering discipline, reviews your application element by element.

Step 5 of 7

Holistic review

The second assessor looks at your application as a whole.

Step 6 of 7

Coordinator review

The IEPNG Coordinator completes a final review and checks.

Step 7 of 7

Endorsement and certificates

The IEPNG Council endorses the application, and you receive your Membership and Registration certificates as a Registered Engineer.

This sequence is taken from IEPNG's published CBA process page. Turnaround for the full assessment is not stated, so ask IEPNG for the current estimate.

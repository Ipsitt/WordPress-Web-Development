Your readiness

Evidence tips for assessors

Assessors judge your own competence, so make it easy to see.

  • Keep every work sample inside your own discipline.
  • Describe what you did, not what the team did.
  • Use evidence you can back up with records.
  • Pick referees who watched your work first-hand.
  • Keep names and dates consistent across all forms.

Exact document lists can change, so confirm against IEPNG's current forms and instructions before you submit.

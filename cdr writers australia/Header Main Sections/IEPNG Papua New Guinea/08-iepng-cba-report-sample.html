IEPNG CBA report sample: a suggested outline for each work sample

Many people search for an IEPNG CBA report example. Use this outline to organise your own evidence.

Section 1

Project snapshot

Name the project, your employer, dates, location and scale in a few lines, so the assessor knows the setting.

Section 2

Your role

List what you personally did: decisions you made, designs you produced and people you supervised.

Section 3

Problem and method

State the engineering problem, the options you weighed and the method or standard you applied, with calculations or data where relevant.

Section 4

Risk, ethics and safety

Show how you managed risk and safety, and how you followed the IEPNG Code of Ethics.

Section 5

Outcome and learning

Describe the result, how it was checked and what you would do differently.

Please note: IEPNG does not publish a model CBA report, and this is a suggested outline, not an IEPNG template. Any IEPNG CBA report sample you find online shows another person's work, so never copy it. Assessors judge your competence, and your referees confirm it.

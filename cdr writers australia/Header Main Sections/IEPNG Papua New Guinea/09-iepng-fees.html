How payment works

Fees are paid upfrontIEPNG processes registration after proof of paymentWork permit application follows registrationRefund can be requested if an issued permit is cancelled early
Check before paying: the fee figures come from an immigration-firm alert that is not dated, and IEPNG's own pages do not list amounts. Confirm current fees and your membership class with IEPNG's Member Services.

IEPNG provisional membership lasts six months. Plan for it.

Provisional Membership and Registration certificates are valid for six months only, and the full assessment must finish inside that window.

Finish in time
  • Submit Stage 2 early, with all forms, work samples and referee forms.
  • Your application gets expedited assessment within the six months.
  • On success you receive Membership and Registration certificates.
If six months pass
  • Provisional Registration or Membership can be cancelled.
  • An expatriate's work permit can be cancelled.
  • Charges can follow under Sections 23 to 26 of the Professional Engineers Registration Act, and the employer can be asked to end employment. Fees are not refunded.

For expatriates, registration is completed first, and only then can the work permit application go in.

IEPNG CBA myths and facts

Six things applicants often get wrong.

Myth

My Engineers Australia CDR will work for IEPNG.

Fact

IEPNG runs its own CBA with its own forms, two work samples and two referees. A CDR is built for a different body.

Myth

A sample report is enough to copy.

Fact

IEPNG publishes no model CBA report, and assessors judge your own competence, backed by your referees.

Myth

IEPNG membership alone lets me practise.

Fact

Practising in PNG also needs registration with the Professional Engineers Registration Board.

Myth

Provisional membership is permanent.

Fact

It lasts six months, and the full assessment must be completed within that time.

Myth

Registration is optional for engineers.

Fact

It is a legal requirement under the Professional Engineers Registration Act.

Myth

Anyone can be my referee.

Fact

Two nominated referees each complete Form CA06 about your work, so pick people who saw it first-hand.

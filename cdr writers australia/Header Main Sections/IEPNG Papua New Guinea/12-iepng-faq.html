IEPNG CBA FAQs: process, forms, fees and report samples

Straight answers to what engineers search before they apply.

What is the IEPNG CBA?
The IEPNG Competency Based Assessment is the process IEPNG uses to assess engineers against its competency standards for membership and registration. It is evidence-based and uses forms, work samples and referees, not a degree check alone.
Is the IEPNG CBA the same as an Engineers Australia CDR?
No. IEPNG's CBA uses IEPNG's own forms (CA01 to CA06), two work samples and two referees. An Engineers Australia CDR is a different report for a different body.
Is there an IEPNG CBA report sample I can follow?
IEPNG does not publish a model CBA report. You can use a suggested outline to organise your own work samples, but you should never copy someone else's sample, because assessors judge your own competence.
Is there an IEPNG CBA report example for work samples?
An example is useful only as a layout guide. A strong work sample describes your project, your own role, the problem and method, risk and ethics, and the outcome, all in your own words and backed by evidence.
How many stages does the IEPNG CBA have?
Two. Stage 1 gives you Provisional Membership after you submit forms CA01 or CA02, CA04 and CA05 and pay the fees. Stage 2 is the full CBA assessment with Form CA03, two work samples and two referees.
How long does provisional membership last?
Provisional Membership and Registration certificates are valid for six months only. IEPNG estimates about two weeks to issue provisional membership once your documents are in order.
What happens if I do not finish within six months?
Your provisional registration or membership can be cancelled, an expatriate's work permit can be cancelled, charges can follow under the Professional Engineers Registration Act, and fees are not refunded.
Who assesses my IEPNG CBA application?
Two assessors review it. Assessor 1 reviews it element by element, matched to your discipline, and Assessor 2 reviews it holistically. The IEPNG Coordinator then does a final review and the IEPNG Council endorses it.
Do I need PERB registration as well as IEPNG membership?
Yes. To practise engineering in PNG you must be registered with the Professional Engineers Registration Board and be a Member of IEPNG. A successful CBA leads to both certificates.
How much does IEPNG registration cost for a foreign engineer?
One immigration-firm alert reports a registration fee of PGK 1,210 and an annual membership fee of PGK 495 per foreign applicant, paid upfront by the employer. IEPNG's own pages do not list amounts, so confirm current fees with IEPNG.
Do I need a work permit before IEPNG registration?
No. For expatriates, registration is completed first, and then the work permit application can be submitted.
What are IEPNG's membership classes?
IEPNG lists Student, Graduate, Technician, Member, Fellow, Honorary Fellow, Structural Engineer and Companion. Most engineers progress from Student to Graduate to Member and later Fellow.

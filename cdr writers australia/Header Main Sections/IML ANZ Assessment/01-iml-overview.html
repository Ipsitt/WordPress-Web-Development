IML skills assessment for senior managers: how the IML ANZ assessment works

The Institute of Managers and Leaders Australia and New Zealand (IML ANZ) is the assessing authority the Australian Government contracts to judge senior-level management skills for skilled migration. An IML skill assessment checks two things together: your higher education qualification and your management employment history, looking back over as much as ten years.

This is separate from IML ANZ membership, and it is a different process from the assessments run by bodies such as VETASSESS. The sections below explain which occupations IML assesses, what it costs, how long it takes and what happens afterwards, so your IML skills assessment is lodged correctly the first time.

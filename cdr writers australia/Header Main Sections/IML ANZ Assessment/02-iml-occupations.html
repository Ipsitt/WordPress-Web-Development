Occupations covered by an IML ANZ skills assessment, with ANZSCO codes

IML ANZ assesses senior-level management roles only. Check that your occupation is on this list before you apply.

One occupation per application

IML will assess a single occupation each time you apply. To switch occupations later, you lodge a reassessment within 12 months of your original decision.

Not on the list?

If your role is not one of these nine, the Department of Home Affairs skilled occupation list shows which authority assesses it.

No immigration advice

IML assesses skills only. It does not advise on visas, so confirm visa requirements with Home Affairs or a registered migration agent.

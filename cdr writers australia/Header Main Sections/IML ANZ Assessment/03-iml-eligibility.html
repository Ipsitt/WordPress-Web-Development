IML ANZ assessment eligibility: who qualifies for an IML skills assessment

Six rules from IML ANZ decide whether your application is worth lodging.

Work experience is essential

IML states that work experience is essential for every occupation it assesses, so graduates without management experience do not qualify.

Degree helps but is not mandatory

Formal qualifications are not compulsory for applicants with long management careers, although the criteria set different experience levels with and without a relevant degree.

Qualification must be comparable

IML judges overseas awards against the Australian Government's Country Education Profiles. Unlisted, unaccredited or not-assessable qualifications are not recognised.

Relevance is checked

Your award certificate and transcript are used to judge how relevant your studies are to the occupation. A marketing degree, for example, may not suit a supply and distribution role.

No English test

IML does not ask for an English language test as part of the skills assessment.

Membership is optional

IML ANZ membership is not required, and a membership grade does not carry over into the migration assessment.

Example: Corporate General Manager (111211)

Each occupation has its own criteria document, so always read yours. For Corporate General Manager, IML lists at least three years in a managerial role plus two years as a Corporate GM if you hold a highly relevant degree, or at least five years in a managerial role plus two years as a Corporate GM without one. You also need at least one year of full-time paid work in the occupation within the past five years. IML notes that employment in an organisation with fewer than 50 staff is unlikely to show the required level.

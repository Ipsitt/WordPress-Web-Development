IML ANZ skills assessment documents checklist

Incomplete or inconsistent documents are the quickest way to slow an IML skill assessment.

  • Award certificateProof of your qualification, used to judge comparability and relevance.
  • Full academic transcriptShows the subjects you studied, which IML uses to judge relevance to your occupation.
  • Employment evidenceDocuments that prove your management roles, dates and level, as listed in the Supporting Document Checklist.
  • Consistent recordsDates, job titles and duties must match across every document, because inconsistencies delay the assessment.
  • Agent authorisation formIf a migration agent acts for you, complete IML's Migration Agent Authorisation Form and submit it after lodging.
The full list is in IML's Supporting Document Checklist PDF, so read it for your occupation before you start. IML cannot give pre-assessment advice or estimate your chance of success, because documents must be assessed and integrity checks completed first.

Official source: IML ANZ frequently asked questions. Details checked 14 October 2026, so confirm current figures with IML ANZ before you apply.

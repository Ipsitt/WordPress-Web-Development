After your IML ANZ outcome: review, reassessment, appeal and renewal

A positive outcome is valid for three years, and some older results for two. Hover over a card to see its fee.

Within 90 days

Review

Ask a different IML assessor to reconsider a Not Suitable outcome within 90 days of the decision.

Fee: $601 outside Australia, $661 inside

Within 12 months

Reassessment

Lodge a new application within 12 months to nominate another occupation or to reapply after gaining experience.

Fee: $601 outside Australia, $661 inside

Within 90 days of review

Appeal

Request an independent review by VETASSESS within 90 days of the review outcome, once review has been used.

Fee: $985 outside Australia, $1,084 inside

Before expiry

Renewal

Apply before your outcome expires to have it reassessed against current criteria. Success is not guaranteed.

Fee: $601 outside Australia, $661 inside

Not sure which one you need? Use a review to challenge an unsuitable result, a reassessment to change occupation or add experience, an appeal only after a review, and a renewal to extend a result that is still valid.

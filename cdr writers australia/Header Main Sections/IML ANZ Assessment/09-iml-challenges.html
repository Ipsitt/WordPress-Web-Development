Common challenges in an IML skills assessment application

Most delays come from the same six problems. Plan for each of them early.

Complex documentation

Management evidence spans several employers and years. A clear structure keeps roles, dates and duties consistent.

Reading the criteria

Each occupation has its own criteria document, and the wrong reading can send you down the wrong path.

Tight deadlines

Visa timelines rarely wait. Planning backwards from the 10-week regular target, or the 6-week express target, protects you.

Changing rules

Fees, timeframes and criteria are updated, so a plan built on last year's figures can fail.

Weak evidence of seniority

Titles alone do not prove a senior role. Reporting lines, team size and decision authority carry the weight.

Unrecognised qualifications

An award that is not comparable to an AQF level is not recognised, which can change your whole strategy.

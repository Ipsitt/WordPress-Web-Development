How we support your IML skill assessment, from first consultation to outcome

Five steps of practical guidance. You stay in control of your own application.

Initial consultation

We review your background, occupation and goals, and check that IML ANZ is the right authority.

Document compilation

We help you gather and organise your management evidence, qualifications and performance records.

Application review

We read your draft against the IML criteria for your occupation and flag gaps before you lodge.

Lodgement support

We guide you through the online IML ANZ application and its deadlines.

Outcome review

We help you read your result and plan next steps, such as review, reassessment or renewal.

What we do

We explain the criteria, organise your evidence, review your drafts and keep you on schedule for your visa plans.

What we cannot do

We do not give immigration advice, predict outcomes or promise approval. IML ANZ alone makes every assessment decision.

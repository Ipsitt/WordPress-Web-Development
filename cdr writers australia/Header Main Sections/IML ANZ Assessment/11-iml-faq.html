IML ANZ assessment FAQs: fees, timeframes, validity and more

Straight answers to what managers ask before they lodge an IML skills assessment.

How much does an IML ANZ skills assessment cost?
From 21 September 2026 the regular assessment costs $820 outside Australia (excluding GST) or $902 inside Australia (including GST). Express adds $218 or $240, and the fee is payable when you lodge.
How long does the IML skills assessment take?
IML's targets are 10 weeks for a regular assessment and 6 weeks for express, counted from a complete application. Incomplete applications take longer.
How long is an IML ANZ assessment valid?
A positive outcome is generally valid for three years from issue, and some older outcomes are valid for two. Your outcome letter states the exact period.
Do I need an English test for an IML assessment?
No. IML does not require an English language test as part of the migration skills assessment.
Can recent graduates apply for an IML skills assessment?
No. IML says work experience is essential for every occupation it assesses, so graduates without management experience do not meet the requirements.
Can I be assessed for more than one occupation?
Not in the same application. IML assesses one occupation each time, and you can lodge a reassessment within 12 months to nominate a different one.
Can I get a refund if I withdraw or if I am unsuccessful?
Fees are not refunded after an unsuccessful outcome. You can withdraw within 2 business days of lodging and receive a refund minus a $50 administration fee.
What is the difference between a review, a reassessment and an appeal?
A review is a reconsideration by a different IML assessor within 90 days. A reassessment is a new application within 12 months, and an appeal is an independent VETASSESS review available only after a review.

KA02 Report Writing

Knowledge Assessment (KA02) is a report prepared by the candidates willing to migrate to New Zealand. Engineering New Zealand, formerly called IPENZ, runs a knowledge assessment for engineers whose qualification is not recognised under the Washington Accord. Applicants and agents often call it the KA02, the KA02 report or the IPENZ KA02. It is not an Engineers Australia document, and it is not a CDR.

Whether you are looking for a KA02 report sample, KA02 report template, or KA02 report example, the goal is always the same: finding a clear way to organize your engineering work so an assessor can easily evaluate your knowledge. Engineering NZ publishes its own guidance and checklist, so the safest approach is to learn the structure and then write about your own projects.

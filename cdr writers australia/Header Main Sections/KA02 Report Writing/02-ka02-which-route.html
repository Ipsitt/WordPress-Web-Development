Do you need a KA02? Choose your qualification route

Engineering NZ decides the route from your credential check. Pick the result closest to yours.

Washington Accord qualification

Engineering NZ states that holders of a Washington Accord qualification, such as an accredited four-year BE, do not need a knowledge assessment for Chartered Member or CPEng. Your credential check confirms the Accord status.

Your credential check result decides the route. It recognises education and study, not skill or experience.

Sydney Accord qualification

For Engineering Technologists with a Sydney Accord three-year degree or equivalent, Engineering NZ evaluates equivalent knowledge as part of the chartership assessment, so a separate KA02 is not the route.

Your credential check result decides the route. It recognises education and study, not skill or experience.

Dublin Accord qualification

For Engineering Technicians with a Dublin Accord two-year qualification or equivalent, equivalent knowledge is likewise looked at within the chartership assessment.

Your credential check result decides the route. It recognises education and study, not skill or experience.

No Accord recognition

If Engineering NZ cannot match your degree to an Accord, you are asked to show equivalent knowledge through a knowledge assessment. This is the route people call KA02.

Your credential check result decides the route. It recognises education and study, not skill or experience.

Chartered overseas

Engineers already chartered with a recognised overseas body may be eligible for an expedited chartered assessment, and they still need to show New Zealand-specific good practice.

Your credential check result decides the route. It recognises education and study, not skill or experience.

Check accreditation, not the country: Accord recognition applies to a specific programme at a specific institution. Run the credential check before you assume which route applies.

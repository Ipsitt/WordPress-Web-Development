KA02 vs CDR: two different documents for two different bodies

They share engineering evidence, but they are written, assessed and filed separately.

≠
Engineering NZ knowledge assessment (KA02)
  • Goes to Engineering New Zealand, applied for through its online portal.
  • Tests whether your knowledge matches a Washington Accord graduate.
  • Supports immigration, Chartered Member or CPEng pathways.
  • Often followed by an interactive discussion with an assessor.
Engineers Australia CDR
  • Goes to Engineers Australia for a migration skills assessment.
  • Built from Career Episodes, a CPD list and a Summary Statement.
  • Used for Australian skilled migration.
  • Does not replace a knowledge assessment for New Zealand.

A positive Engineers Australia assessment can sometimes be shown to Engineering NZ as a recognised credential during the credential check. Your project stories may overlap, but the document formats do not.

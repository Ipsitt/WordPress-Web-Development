The Engineering NZ knowledge assessment process, step by step

Follow a KA02 application from credential check to decision.

Step 1 of 6

Check your qualification

Create an account in the Engineering NZ portal, add each credential with evidence and complete the ID check. It takes typically 7 to 10 working days after payment, and is free for financial members.

Step 2 of 6

Standard or fast track

Standard applies to immigration and chartered purposes. Fast track, introduced in 2023, is for people who need a knowledge assessment for immigration.

Step 3 of 6

Submit through the portal

Knowledge assessment applications go through Engineering NZ's online portal. Questions go to assessment@engineeringnz.org.

Step 4 of 6

Your evidence is reviewed

An assessor reviews your application against the Washington Accord knowledge standard.

Step 5 of 6

Possible interactive assessment

Specialist sites report a video discussion of about 60 to 90 minutes, and sometimes extra challenge tests. Confirm current practice with Engineering NZ.

Step 6 of 6

You are told the outcome

A positive result shows equivalent knowledge. If gaps are found, you are told which areas to address.

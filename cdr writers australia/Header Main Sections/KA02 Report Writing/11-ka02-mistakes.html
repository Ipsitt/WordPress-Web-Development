Common KA02 Mistakes that Slow an Engineering NZ Application

Six errors to avoid before you submit.

01

Assuming KA02 is for everyone

Washington Accord qualification holders do not need a knowledge assessment, so check your credential first.

02

Mixing it up with a CDR

A KA02 goes to Engineering NZ and a CDR goes to Engineers Australia. They are not interchangeable.

03

Describing the team

Assessors want your own contribution, written in the first person.

04

Gaps in the Knowledge Profile

If one knowledge area is never shown, the assessor has nothing to credit.

05

Copying a sample

Engineering NZ treats plagiarism and fraud seriously, so use samples only as a layout guide.

06

Using old fees and timelines

Fees and process pages change. Check the current page before you budget.

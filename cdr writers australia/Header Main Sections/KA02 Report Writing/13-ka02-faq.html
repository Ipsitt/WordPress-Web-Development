KA02 FAQs: sample, template, fees and Engineering NZ process

Straight answers to what engineers search before they apply.

What is the KA02 report?
KA02 is the name people use for the Engineering New Zealand knowledge assessment. It shows that your engineering knowledge is equivalent to a Washington Accord graduate when your degree is not recognised under the Accord.
Is there a KA02 report sample I can follow?
Engineering NZ does not publish a model KA02 report. A suggested outline can help you organise your own evidence, but you must never copy another applicant's work.
Where can I find a KA02 report template?
Engineering NZ provides guidance and a checklist rather than a fill-in template. Use a structure such as personal details, education, work samples, Knowledge Profile matrix, CPD record and supporting evidence.
Is there a KA02 report example for work samples?
An example is useful only as a layout guide. A strong work sample explains the project, your own role, the technical work, the knowledge areas shown and the outcome, in your own words.
What does knowledge assessment Engineering NZ mean?
It is Engineering New Zealand's assessment of whether your engineering knowledge matches a Washington Accord graduate. It sits between the credential check and chartered competence assessment.
Is IPENZ the same as Engineering NZ?
Yes. IPENZ is the former name of Engineering New Zealand, which is why many people still say IPENZ KA02.
Do I need a KA02 if I hold a Washington Accord degree?
No. Engineering NZ states that Washington Accord qualification holders do not need a knowledge assessment.
What is the difference between KA02 and a CDR?
A KA02 is assessed by Engineering NZ. A CDR is assessed by Engineers Australia for Australian migration. They share project evidence but not format.
How much does the Engineering NZ knowledge assessment cost?
Engineering NZ lists $1,425 for standard and $1,977 for fast track, both excluding GST. The credential check is $550 for non-members and free for financial members.
How long does it take?
The credential check typically takes 7 to 10 working days after payment. Engineering NZ does not publish a single turnaround for the knowledge assessment on the pages we reviewed, so check with it directly.
Can I use fast track?
Fast track is for people who need a knowledge assessment for immigration purposes. Standard covers immigration and chartered purposes.
Is KA02 the same as CPEng?
No. KA02 shows equivalent knowledge. CPEng is a registration that assesses competence and needs New Zealand engineering experience.

Steps to follow while preparing your KA02 Report

KA02 report assessment takes typically 6-8 weeks. However, it also depends on the content of the report. If they find mistakes, you will be asked to re-submit your report, which will delay your processing. So before submitting recheck your report correctly to avoid such errors and get your report result in no-time.

Initially, prepare a draft on how you will be writing your report. If possible, divide your reports into parts and deal with them one by one. This will clear your path and give you significant results.

Learn the IPENZ guidelines

IPENZ provides rules and steps for candidate to prepare their KA02 report. So make sure to go through the rule book and other references provided by the official body before finalizing.

Report writing

The knowledge of report writing that you gained from educational years should be utilized in your report. Technical as well as grammatical aspects both should be covered along with simple vocabulary.

Expert Help What is NER Australia Prerequisites of NER Our NER Services National Engineering Register (NER Australia), which is nothing but the registration framework introduced by Engineers Australia. NER is also called the Register of National Engineers. NER plays a special role for Engineers in getting their recognition. Some below points must be noted while writing the National Engineering Register (NER) statement The work experience statement must be written in chronological order. Recent and relevant experience matching the required skills must be presented first to gain the attention The area of practicing must have at least 5 years of experience. Role and critical achievements and responsibilities should be essentially mentioned. You can mention the past position in short if the work experience is too lengthy to portray properly. Who is eligible to get their registration to the NER? Chartered members of Engineers Australia in each occupational category of Engineers. Members of Engineers Australia in each occupation role. Non-EA members Who have relevant experience in their field of practice for 5 years.

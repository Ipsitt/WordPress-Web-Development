P.Eng competency assessment in Canada: how the competency-based assessment (CBA) works

To earn a Professional Engineer (P.Eng) licence in Canada, you must prove that your engineering work has made you competent to practise safely. Most provincial regulators now do this through a competency-based assessment (CBA). Instead of listing years and job titles, you write real work examples, rate yourself, and have validators who watched you work confirm and score them.

The rules are not identical in every province. Ontario, British Columbia, Alberta and Newfoundland and Labrador each set their own experience minimums, competency counts and validator rules. The sections below compare them side by side, so you know what your P.Eng competency report must actually show.

The seven P.Eng competency categories and the minimum ratings you must reach

Each competency is rated from 0 to 5. Assessors then average your ratings in every category, and each average must reach a minimum.

1. Technical Competenceminimum average 3.0 out of 5
2. Communicationminimum average 3.0 out of 5
3. Project and Financial Managementminimum average 2.0 out of 5
4. Team Effectivenessminimum average 3.0 out of 5
5. Professional Accountabilityminimum average 3.0 out of 5
6. Social, Economic, Environmental and Sustainabilityminimum average 2.0 out of 5
7. Personal Continuing Professional Developmentminimum average 3.0 out of 5

Most competencies need a 1

PEO says most individual competencies need a rating of at least 1, while its six professional standards competencies need a 2 or a 3.

You are not expected to be at 4 or 5

EGBC states that applicants are not expected to be working at an advanced professional level, which is a rating of 4 or 5.

Alberta scores differently

APEGA uses 22 competencies in six categories, with its own scoring and minimums, so do not copy another province's table.

The minimum averages above are those published by PEO and EGBC.

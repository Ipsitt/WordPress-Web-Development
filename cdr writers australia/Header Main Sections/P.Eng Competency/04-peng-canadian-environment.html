Canadian environment competencies: the eight that decide whether overseas experience counts

EGBC lists eight competencies that must each meet their minimum rating. Hover over a card to see what to show.

1.1

Regulations, Codes and Standards

Minimum rating 3.0

Show you applied the codes and standards that govern your work.

1.6

Safety Awareness

Minimum rating 3.0

Show how you identified and managed safety risks.

1.9

Peer Review and Quality Control

Minimum rating 3.0

Show how your work was checked and how you checked others.

2.1

Oral Communication

Minimum rating 3.0

Show clear spoken communication in English or French.

2.2

Writing

Minimum rating 3.0

Show clear written communication in English or French.

2.3

Reading and Comprehension

Minimum rating 3.0

Show you can read and act on technical and regulatory documents.

5.1

Code of Ethics

Minimum rating 3.0

Show how the code of ethics shaped a real decision.

6.2

Engineering and the Public

Minimum rating 2.0

Show awareness of how your work affects the public.

Important: if any one of these is rated below its minimum, EGBC asks you to revise the example whatever your category average is. International experience does not automatically meet them, so explain how your overseas work was equivalent.

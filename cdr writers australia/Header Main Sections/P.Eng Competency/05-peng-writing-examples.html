How to write P.Eng competency examples using the Situation, Action, Outcome method

PEO, EGBC and APEGA all ask for work examples that show what you personally did. Hover over a step.

Situation

Give a brief overview of the specific project or problem, including the context, the constraints and your role.

Action

This is usually the longest part. Describe what you did, the engineering judgements you made, the methods and the reasons, in the first person.

Outcome

Explain the result and its impact, and quantify it wherever you can, such as savings, performance gains or safety results.

Use 'I'write about your own actions, not what 'we' did
Be specificavoid phrases like 'participated in' or 'was involved with'
Within 7 yearsuse recent examples where you can (EGBC guidance)

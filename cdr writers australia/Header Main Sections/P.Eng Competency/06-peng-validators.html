EGBC validators and references: who can confirm your competency examples

Your examples only count once someone who watched you work has confirmed and scored them.

Who can validate

EGBC expects validators to have first-hand knowledge of your engineering work, so pick people who supervised you or worked alongside you on the projects you describe. At least one must be a supervisor in the same or a related discipline.

How many you need

EGBC asks for at least four validators, and together they must cover at least four years of your experience. Plan your list early so every period of your history has someone to confirm it.

What they do

Validators confirm each competency example, rate it on the same 0 to 5 scale independently of you, and add comments. Assessors then make the final decision on your application.

Working overseas?

Overseas validators are normal, but they must still have first-hand knowledge of your work. Brief them early on the 0 to 5 scale and on writing comments that explain their score, because vague confirmations slow your file down.

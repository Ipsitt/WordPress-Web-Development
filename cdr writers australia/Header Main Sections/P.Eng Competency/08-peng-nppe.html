National Professional Practice Examination (NPPE) for P.Eng applicants

The NPPE tests professionalism, law and ethics. You sit it in addition to your competency assessment.

110multiple-choice questions, four options each
2.5 hoursexam time, per PEO's candidate guide
65scaled pass score, which is not 65% correct
Remotecomputer-based with online proctoring

Fees and attempts

Each regulator sets its own fee. APEGA charges $250 plus GST per exam, allows a maximum of four attempts, and lets you register only after you submit your application. Late registrations are not accepted.

Details that vary

Some jurisdictions, such as BC, add an essay and run a longer exam. EGBC says results appear on the status portal about three to four weeks after the exam, and that a booked proctoring session cannot be cancelled or deferred.

Check your regulator and the NPPE site for the current format, dates and fee.

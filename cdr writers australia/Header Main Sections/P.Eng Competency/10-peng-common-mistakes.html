Common P.Eng competency report mistakes that get examples sent back

Avoid these six and you remove most of the reasons assessors return a submission.

Writing 'we' instead of 'I'

Assessors cannot credit you for what your team did. Show your own decisions and actions.

Vague verbs

Phrases such as 'participated in' or 'was involved with' give assessors nothing to verify.

Rating below the minimum

If your self-rating is under the required minimum, you are asked to revise it or gain more experience.

Unexplained gaps and overlaps

EGBC asks you to explain gaps or overlaps in your history, and APEGA may assess extra experience after a gap of more than seven years.

Outsourced or AI-written examples

EGBC states that using AI or third parties to create or embellish examples is strictly prohibited and can lead to disciplinary action.

Unverifiable claims

Assessors cannot rely on implied evidence, so every claim needs enough detail for a validator to confirm.

Know the rules on outside help

Regulators expect the examples to describe your own work in your own words. We therefore focus on guidance, structure and review, and the technical content must come from you.

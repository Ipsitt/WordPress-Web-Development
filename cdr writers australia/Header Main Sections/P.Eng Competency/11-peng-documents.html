P.Eng application documents checklist for Canadian regulators

Gather these early, because missing records and validator delays slow most files.

  • Academic recordsOfficial transcripts and your degree certificate. International graduates in Ontario need a WES course-by-course report.
  • Two government IDsEGBC asks for a certified copy of two government-issued IDs, one with a photo, as a single PDF.
  • Employment historyEmployers, positions and dates, with any gaps or overlaps explained in writing.
  • Competency examples and ratingsYour Situation, Action, Outcome examples with a self-rating for each required competency.
  • Validator detailsNames and contact details for each validator, including overseas validators, plus their licence details where relevant.
  • English evidence, only if askedEGBC may ask for an IELTS, CAEL or Duolingo English Test result, and says not to send one unless requested.
Each regulator publishes its own checklist and good character process, so use your regulator's list as the final word.

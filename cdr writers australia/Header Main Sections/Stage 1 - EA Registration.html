Introduction to Stage 1 Competency Assessment

It is the first level of competency that ensures whether you are able to practice engineering in Australia or not. It should cover your education qualification and knowledge of relevant job positions such that you can become an eligible member.

In this context, you must know that your job posting will be based on four different occupational categories. This includes Engineering Technologist, Professional Engineer, Engineering Associate and Engineering Manager. For each of these posts, there is a specific requirement to be fulfilled. If you have read the instructions carefully and has the ability to apply for any of these posts, you must send your CDR report in the appropriate format.

TRA Application Format & Requirements

The TRA assessment process generally involves reviewing:

Eligibility Checklist

  • Correct TRA pathway selection
  • Verified trade qualification or apprenticeship
  • Post-qualification employment experience
  • Practical skills alignment with occupation standards
  • Proper documentation format
We provide guidance to ensure your documentation aligns with the correct occupation category requirements.

Key Documents Typically Required

  • Trade qualification certificates
  • Apprenticeship completion evidence (if applicable)
  • Employment reference letters
  • Payslips / taxation records (if required)
  • Resume/CV
  • Identity documents
  • Practical assessment evidence (for certain pathways)
We provide guidance to ensure your documentation aligns with the correct occupation category requirements.

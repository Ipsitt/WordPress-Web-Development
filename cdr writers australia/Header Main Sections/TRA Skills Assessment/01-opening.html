Your outcome rests on your paperwork

Under the Migration Skills Assessment, TRA judges your qualification and work history from what you upload. There is no hearing where you can explain a gap, so your certificates, employer statements and pay records must make your case on their own.

Other TRA programs work differently, and some include a technical assessment, so confirm which program applies to you before you start. TRA's programs cover employer-sponsored visas as well as permanent skilled migration.

About Trades Recognition Australia

An Australian Government skills assessment service for people with trade skills who want to migrate.

Trades Recognition Australia (TRA) assesses people with trade skills, whether gained overseas or in Australia, who want to migrate. It runs several programs, and the right one depends on your occupation, your passport country, where you studied and the visa you are seeking.

Because so many trades fall under TRA, it is usually the first assessing authority overseas tradespeople meet. Understanding how its programs work before you apply gives you a head start.

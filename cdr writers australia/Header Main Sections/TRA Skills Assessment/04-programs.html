Which TRA program fits you?

Applying under the wrong program wastes time and can hurt your result. TRA says the right one depends on your occupation, passport country, place of study and visa type.

Migration Skills Assessment

For people applying for a permanent skilled visa in occupations and countries that the other programs do not cover. A successful outcome letter goes to the Department of Home Affairs with your visa application.

Offshore Skills Assessment Program

For applicants for a skilled visa other than the 485 or TSS, where this program applies to their country and occupation.

Job Ready Program

For international graduates in Australia who hold an Australian trade or associate professional qualification from a registered training organisation. A Provisional Skills Assessment has to be completed first.

TSS Skills Assessment Program

For people nominated for a Temporary Skill Shortage visa (subclass 482) in a particular occupation.

Migration Points Advice

A service Home Affairs may require so it can decide the points an applicant receives.

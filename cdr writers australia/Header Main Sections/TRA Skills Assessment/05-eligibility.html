Eligibility for the Migration Skills Assessment

TRA weighs your occupation, qualification and work history together, and you must satisfy each.

Occupation

Your nominated trade must appear on the MLTSSL or STSOL, with TRA as its assessing authority, and connect directly to your qualification, apprenticeship or employment. You cannot use this program if the Offshore, Job Ready or TSS program covers you instead.

Qualification

A finished qualification or apprenticeship, obtained before you apply and comparable to the Australian one for your trade. Evidence: final certificate, a full transcript with dates and program details, and apprenticeship records where relevant.

Work experience

Three years of full-time-equivalent paid work directly relevant to the trade, before, during or after your qualification, with 12 months of it in the three years before you apply.

Not accepted in place of a qualification

  • Occupational licences
  • Trade tests without formal training
  • Short courses or work experience alone
  • Incomplete qualifications or statements of attainment

Tip for your employer statements

Describe your own duties in your own words. TRA does not want task lists copied from ANZSCO or other outside sources.

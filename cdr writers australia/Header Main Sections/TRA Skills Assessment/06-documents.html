Documents for your TRA application

One missing or weak document can slow the assessment or sink it, so build your file from this list.

Key documents required

  • PassportThe identification page.
  • Qualification evidenceFinal certificate, a full transcript showing dates and program details, and apprenticeship records where they apply.
  • Employer TemplateA completed template for every period of employment you claim.
  • Pay evidenceAt least two verifiable sources for each calendar year, such as tax records, payslips, superannuation records or bank statements showing salary deposits.
  • Self-employment evidenceAt least three kinds of document for each calendar year, such as business registration, invoices, bank statements or tax records.

File and wording rules

  • File formatFull-colour PDFs at 150 dpi or better, each under 10MB. Sharing links, locked or password-protected files and handwritten employment documents are refused.
  • TranslationsAnything not in English needs an English translation. In Australia it must come from a NAATI-accredited translator whose name, NAATI number and status appear on it.
  • Overseas translationsNeed approval from the authorities in that country, the organisation's letterhead, an official stamp and the translator's name, signature and phone number.
  • Your own wordsWrite task descriptions yourself or have your employer write them. Do not lift them from ANZSCO.

The TRA application in five steps

The process follows a fixed order. Knowing it lets you plan, especially if a visa deadline is running.

Confirm your occupation

Check that your trade is on the relevant occupation list and that TRA is the assessing authority.

Pick your program

Choose the one that fits your occupation, passport, place of study and visa. Choosing wrongly delays everything.

Prepare your documents

Gather everything in the required format before you open the online form.

Apply online

Complete the TRA portal form, upload your files and pay the fee. The Migration Skills Assessment fee is $795 AUD.

Wait for the outcome

TRA emails the result. A successful outcome letter goes to Home Affairs with your visa application.

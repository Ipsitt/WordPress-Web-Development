Mistakes that sink TRA applications

Most unsuccessful applications trace back to fixable paperwork problems. Watch for these six.

Vague duties

Employer statements that skip specific trade tasks, or tasks copied from ANZSCO.

Unrelated experience

Roles that do not truly match the nominated trade.

No finished qualification

Relying on a licence, trade test, short course or statement of attainment instead.

Too little experience

Under three years in total, or without 12 months in the last three years.

Thin pay evidence

Fewer than two verifiable sources in a calendar year.

File problems

Unreadable scans, wrong format, handwritten employment documents or missing translations.

If the result is unsuccessful

TRA sends reasons. You can ask for a review within 28 days for $610 AUD, which is refunded only if the outcome turns favourable on the original evidence alone. Or you can lodge a new application, resubmitting all evidence and paying the fee again.

A caution

False or misleading information can lead TRA to revoke an outcome and refuse future applications for up to three years.

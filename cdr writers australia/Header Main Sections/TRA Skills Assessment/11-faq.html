TRA Skill Assessment FAQs

Questions we hear all the time, answered clearly.

Who needs a TRA skills assessment?
Tradespeople applying for a skilled migration visa, or for certain employer-sponsored visas, generally need one. The result is the evidence the Department of Home Affairs looks for.
What qualification do I need?
A completed trade qualification or apprenticeship that is comparable to the Australian one for your occupation, obtained before you apply.
How long does it take?
TRA generally finalises the Migration Skills Assessment within 120 days of online submission. A complete first submission avoids delays.
How much does it cost?
The Migration Skills Assessment fee is $795 AUD and a review is $610 AUD. Fees are not refunded if you withdraw after the assessment starts. Check TRA's site for current amounts.
Can I apply without a formal qualification?
The Migration Skills Assessment needs a completed qualification, and a licence or trade test alone is not accepted. TRA also runs a documentary evidence pathway for licensed trades, which asks for four or six years of experience depending on formal training and can include a technical assessment. It is a pilot, so confirm that it is open and suits your trade.
What if my assessment is unsuccessful?
TRA sends written reasons. You can request a review within 28 days or lodge a new application with stronger evidence. A document review before resubmitting can help you spot the weak points.

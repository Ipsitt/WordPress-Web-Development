Why a VETASSESS skill assessment matters for your migration

For many general professional occupations, a positive VETASSESS skills assessment is the first checkpoint before a skilled visa or nomination can move forward.

Verified skills

Your qualifications and employment are compared with Australian standards for your nominated occupation.

Visa requirement

Where VETASSESS is the assessing authority for your occupation, a suitable outcome is needed before the related application.

Professional credibility

A suitable VETASSESS skills assessment shows that your experience meets Australian benchmarks.

Occupation recognition

Roles such as marketing specialist, management consultant and café or restaurant manager are assessed against defined criteria.

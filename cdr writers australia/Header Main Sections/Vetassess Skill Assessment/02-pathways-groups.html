Professional or trade? Choose the right VETASSESS pathway

Your occupation decides which route applies. The wrong pathway is a common and avoidable delay.

A professional VETASSESS skills assessment reviews your qualification against the Australian Qualifications Framework and your employment for relevance and skill level. Occupations sit in groups by ANZSCO skill level.

Groups A and B

Skill level 1

Australian bachelor, master or doctoral degree level.

Groups C and E

Skill level 2

AQF associate degree, advanced diploma or diploma level.

Group D

Skill level 3

AQF Certificate III (with extra years of employment) or Certificate IV.

You must meet one pathway fully on its own. Criteria from different pathways cannot be combined, and highly relevant work cannot replace a missing qualification at the required level.

Separate process

Trade assessments follow their own program rules, evidence and timeframes, which differ from the professional route.

Fees paid to TRA

For trades assessed by Trades Recognition Australia, fees and refunds are handled directly by TRA, so read its guidelines first.

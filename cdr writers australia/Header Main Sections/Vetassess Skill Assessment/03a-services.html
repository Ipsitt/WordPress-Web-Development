Our VETASSESS skills assessment support services

From choosing your occupation to answering follow-up requests, we support every stage of your VETASSESS skills assessment.

Occupation consultation

We review your study and career history and match it to the most suitable ANZSCO occupation.

Document compilation and review

Certificates, transcripts, employer statements and ID are checked against what VETASSESS expects.

Application preparation

We help you complete each section of the online application so your duties are described clearly.

CV and supporting statements

A CV is mandatory for a formal assessment. We structure yours to show your real capabilities.

Employment evidence guidance

We guide you and your employer on a complete Statement of Service and matching payment evidence.

Follow-up support

If VETASSESS asks for more information, we help you respond accurately and on time.

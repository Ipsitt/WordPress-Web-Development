Your readiness

What a strong Statement of Service shows

Written on letterhead and signed by your manager, supervisor or HR representative, it should state:

Exact start and finish dates, the issue date, weekly hours, employment type, job titles, a detailed list of duties, the business name and address, and the referee's name, position, contact details and signature.

Payment evidence (at least one)

Two or more payslips, government tax records, superannuation documents, or bank statements showing employer deposits. For jobs longer than three years, include payslips from the start, middle and end.

A statutory declaration can explain gaps but is supporting evidence only. It cannot replace employer or financial records, or prove employment dates and pay. Self-employed applicants need business registration, payment evidence and a declaration of duties.

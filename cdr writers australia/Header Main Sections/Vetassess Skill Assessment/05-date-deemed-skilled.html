Does VETASSESS deduct experience? Understanding the Date Deemed Skilled

In effect, yes. The qualifying period of highly relevant work needed to pass is not counted again for points test advice. Your history is not erased; only work after your Date Deemed Skilled earns points advice.

Qualifying period10 Jan 2019 to 9 Jan 2020

These months show you meet the assessment criteria, which typically means at least one year of highly relevant work at the required skill level within the five years before you lodge. They are not counted again as skilled employment for points.

Date Deemed Skilled10 Jan 2020

The day after the qualifying period is met. VETASSESS works this date out for you and uses it as the starting line for your points test advice.

Points test advice10 Jan 2020 to 9 Jan 2025

Only employment from this date counts as skilled employment in the advice. The five-year window is just an example, and the Department of Home Affairs makes the final decision on points.

At least one year

You generally need a minimum of one year of highly relevant employment at the required skill level within the five years before you lodge.

Longer in some cases

If a qualification is not highly relevant, more years of highly relevant work may be needed. Check your occupation information sheet.

Waiting costs nothing

Your employment is considered from the day you lodge, so processing time does not shrink your experience.

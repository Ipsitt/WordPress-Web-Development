Expiry, outcomes and your VETASSESS login

Know how long your result lasts, what happens if it is not suitable, and where to follow your application.

Does VETASSESS expire?

Yes. A skills assessment is currently valid for three years from its issue date for migration. Renew within those three years at a lower fee; renewals typically take 2 to 5 weeks. Confirm visa-specific rules with the Department of Home Affairs.

If the outcome is not suitable

Your letter explains which components fell short. You can request a review within 90 days or a reassessment within 12 months, and an appeal is also available. Talk to the assessor first.

VETASSESS login

Use the VETASSESS login on the VETASSESS website to upload evidence, track your application, download your signed declaration page and access your outcome letter.

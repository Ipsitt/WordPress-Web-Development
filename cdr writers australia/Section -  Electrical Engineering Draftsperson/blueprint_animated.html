Electrical Engineering Draftsperson

Electrical engineering draftspersons create detailed diagrams, blueprints, and circuit layouts used to manufacture electrical equipment and systems for communications, power plants, and electrical distribution. Working closely with engineers, they collect field data, run technical computations, perform tests, and graph analytical results.

While they occasionally collaborate in workshops or testing laboratories, they primarily work in an office setting, spending substantial time utilizing computer-aided design (CAD) software and handling supporting administrative planning.

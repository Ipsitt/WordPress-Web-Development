Tools and Technologies

Electrical engineering draftspeople use computers, especially CAD (computer-aided design) software, as well as electrical system modeling and testing systems. They must also be knowledgeable with various types of electrical circuits and parts, such as transformers, circuit breakers, and transmission lines. They may be expected to utilize other office equipment as well.

Visa Program Eligibility

Because ANZSCO 312312 – Electrical Engineering Technician is featured on the Medium and Long‑term Strategic Skills List (MLTSSL), it opens pathways across General Skilled Migration and employer‑sponsored programmes.

  • Skilled Independent Visa (subclass 189)
  • Skilled Nominated Visa (subclass 190) – requires state/territory nomination
  • Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) Visa (subclass 491) – regional pathway
  • Employer‑sponsored visas – Skills in Demand (subclass 482) and Employer Nomination Scheme (subclass 186)

A positive skills assessment from Trades Recognition Australia (TRA) is required, along with meeting the standard Australian health, character and English language requirements.

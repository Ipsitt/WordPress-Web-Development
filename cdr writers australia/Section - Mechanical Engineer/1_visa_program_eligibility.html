Visa Program Eligibility

Because 233511 is featured on the Medium and Long-term Strategic Skills List (MLTSSL), it opens pathways across General Skilled Migration and employer-sponsored programs:

Eligible Visas:

Subclass 189 (Skilled Independent)
Subclass 190 (Skilled Nominated)
Subclass 491 (Work Regional - State & Family)
Subclass 482 (Skills in Demand - Core Skills Stream)
Subclass 186 (ENS - Direct Entry)
Subclass 494 (Regional Employer Sponsored)
Subclass 485 (Post-Higher Education Work Stream)
Subclass 407 (Training Visa)

Ineligible Streams:

Subclass 482 Skills in Demand Specialist Skills Stream — Not applicable at this skill/salary band
Subclass 489 (Superseded permanently by Subclass 491)
Subclass 187 RSMS (Closed / Superseded by Subclass 494)
